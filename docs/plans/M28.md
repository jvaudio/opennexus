# M28 · Present legend fit

## Plan

**Goal (closing M21's Present gaps):** the corner legend never covers a slide's topics,
it lists only what the slide shows, and flowchart, DFD and sequence decks get Timeline
slides too.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Room for the legend:** a slide whose topics reach the stage's bottom-left
  corner is framed so that no framed topic is under the legend, on screen (with and
  without the strip) and in exported slides. The legend stays clear of the progress
  dots. Slides the legend doesn't touch are framed exactly as before.
- **A2 · The slide's own legend:** each slide's legend lists only the markers and labels
  on that slide's topics, in the legend's order and with its names; a slide with none
  has no legend. VoiceOver reads what's shown.
- **A3 · Exports:** PDF, PowerPoint, Keynote and video slides draw the same per-slide
  legend, framed the same way.
- **A4 · Timeline slides for diagrams:** flow and message decks get a Timeline slide when
  Timeline slides are on and the diagram has dated tasks, or the reason they don't is
  recorded.
- **A5 · Quality bars:** no new strings outside Loc (catalog regenerated), no new
  animation, no network APIs, no package dependencies, and `.nexus` files are unchanged.

### Room for the legend: reserve, don't move (D232, D236)

- **Where the legend goes:** it stays bottom-left. Moving it to the emptiest corner was
  the alternative, but a box that jumps between slides is one more thing for the
  audience to find, and the other corners are taken (the title, the progress dots, and a
  wrapped title across the top).
- **How the slide is framed:** fit the topics to the whole stage as before; if a framed
  topic then comes within 6 pt of the legend's box, refit to the stage above the legend
  or to its right (12 pt clear), whichever zooms larger, moving from the centre only as
  far as needed. `SlideFit` is pure, so it is tested directly.
- **One place for the box:** `PresentLegendPlacement` gives the overlay, the framing and
  the exported slide the same rectangle (1.2× and 28 pt above the strip on screen; 2×,
  84 pt in and 64 pt up on a 1920 × 1080 slide).
- **The dots:** they and the legend now share a line just above the strip. Since M5 the
  dots sat 268 pt above the strip, in the middle of the stage.

### The slide's own legend (D233, D234)

- A slide's topics are the ones it frames (`Slide.focus`), less topics hidden from
  slides and topics under a fold. `SceneLegend.make(…, among:)` keeps the legend's rows
  whose marker or label one of them carries.
- No rows, no legend, and nothing to frame around. The canvas legend's "No markers or
  labels yet" row never reaches a slide.
- Exports draw the slides with the same rule; L and the Legend chip stay deck-wide.

### Timeline slides for diagrams (D235)

- Flow and message decks close with one `timeline:<root>` slide charting every dated
  task, titled "Forgot password · Timeline". Hierarchies keep one per branch.

### Built

- **NexusCore:** `Deck.diagramTimeline`, closing flow and message decks.
- **NexusCanvas:**
  - `PresentExtras.swift`: `PresentLegendPlacement`, `SlideFit`,
    `CanvasView.presentLegendRect()`, `slideTopics`, per-slide `presentLegend(for:)`,
    `timelineTopics`, and the export's legend drawn from the shared placement.
  - `LegendViews.swift`: `SceneLegend.make(…, among:)`.
  - `PresentViews.swift`: the legend and dots on one line above the strip;
    `SlideStripView.height`.
  - `CanvasView.frame` fits slides with `SlideFit`; `slideLegendPlacement` for exports.
  - `SlideExporter.renderSlides` takes an optional `inspect` callback for tests.

### Known gaps

- **Only framed topics are kept clear.** Dimmed context outside a branch slide's framing,
  zone outlines and lines can still pass under the legend.
- **A Timeline slide's chart covers the top of the stage.** A diagram's Timeline frames
  the whole diagram, so on a tall flowchart the chart sits over its upper half, and the
  topmost topic can touch the title, as on the title slide. Branch Timelines share this
  when a branch is tall.
- **The legend and the dots can meet** in a window too narrow for both: a 300 pt legend
  and a deck of 60 or more slides at under about 1,200 pt wide.
- **The Present strip's chips are still mouse targets** (M21); the keyboard uses L and T.
