### Every page (D190)

- **`ImageText.recognize`** returns one `RecognisedImage` per page: every page of a PDF,
  and every image in a multi-page TIFF (a GIF's frames are animation, read as one
  picture), up to 100 pages. Every page of a PDF renders at one scale, so text sizes
  compare across pages. A `progress` callback gets (pages read, pages to read), and
  cancellation is checked between pages.
- **`ImageOutline.build(pages:title:)`** returns an `ImageImport`:
  - Page numbers ("12", "Page 3", "4 of 9") in the top or bottom 6% of a page are left
    out. So is text repeated in that margin on at least half the pages, numbers aside
    (a running header or footer).
  - Pages with no text are skipped and counted.
  - **The pages continue one document** when every page reads as a list and none
    after the first opens with a title: a first line at least 1.5× the body text,
    measured without each page's first line. The pages are then stacked and read as one
    list, so a heading's points that carry over to the next page stay under it.
  - **Otherwise each page is a canvas**: slides, posters, or a map or fishbone per page.
    A page with its own title names its canvas, and otherwise the canvas is
    "name · page N".
- **The report** counts the source's pages as sheets. It lists unreadable marks, "Page
  numbers, headers and footers", "Pages with no text" and "Pages after page 100".
- **Where it lands:**
  - File › Import… opens a document with every canvas.
  - A drop or ⇧⌘V adds them all after the current canvas, as one undo step. An untouched
    canvas takes the first page.
  - The merge sheet and `nexus import` get every page, through the same
    `ImageOutline.read`.

### Off the main thread (D191)

- **The merge sheet:** adding a picture or PDF adds its row at once, showing "Reading the
  text in the image…" and a progress bar ("Reading page 2 of 5…" for a PDF). The text
  is read in a detached task through `ImageToMap.importImage`, the same path as File ›
  Import…, so Settings › AI › Group plain lists applies here too.
  - Merge stays disabled until every picture is read.
  - Removing the row or closing the sheet cancels the reading between pages.
  - `addMergeSource` returns the task, so tests can await it.
- **A drop or ⇧⌘V** shows "Reading page N of M…" toasts for a PDF of several pages.

### Fishbone diagrams (D192)

`Builder.fishbone()` runs before the other passes. It tries a head at either end:
- **The head** is the text furthest to that side, at least median size, in the middle
  half of the picture's height. Its middle is the spine.
- **The rules:** everything sits behind the head, and at most one line in 20 crosses the
  spine.
- **The fit:** working outward from the spine, each line either joins the nearest bone
  in front of it or starts a new bone as its label. A bone leaves its label's edge
  nearest the spine and leans toward the head. Joining means the line's head-side end is
  behind the bone, by no more than 3 lines (or in front by half a line), and reaches to
  within a line of the label's far edge.
  - Slopes from 0.2 to 1.6 are tried. The one with the fewest bones, then the most even
    gaps, wins.
- **Accepted only when:**
  - there are two or more bones, with at least one on each side of the spine;
  - at least two causes, and at least half the bones have one;
  - the gaps vary by no more than ¾ of a line (standard deviation);
  - every bone with two or more causes leans: its causes shift toward the head by at
    least half the slope as they near the spine;
  - no two labels on one side share a column.
  These rules keep one-sided maps, braces and timelines out.
- **The result:** categories sit in the order their bones meet the spine, nearest the head
  first, and the upper bone of a pair comes first. Causes go from the label inward,
  which is OpenNexus's own fishbone order. The canvas is a `fishbone` with
  `fishbone.left` (head on the right) or `fishbone.right` structure.

### Connector lines (D193)

- **`InkMap`** marks the cells that differ from the picture's background (the commonest
  colour on its edges) by more than a faint fill does. It's at most 1,200 cells on the
  longest side, a cell set when any of its pixels is ink. It's built for every page.
  - A picture that's 30% ink or more (a photo) isn't traced.
- **`Tracer.parent(of:side:accepts:)`**, in the map pass:
  - It starts from ink just inside a topic's inner edge (where its border or underline
    meets its connector) and walks the ink breadth first.
  - It never goes further out than that edge, and never through text.
  - It stops at the first topic the geometry would accept as a parent (already placed,
    and between the topic and the centre). That topic must be reached on its side facing
    the child, within 0.8 of a line of its text.
- **What decides:** a traced parent outranks the geometric one. With no trace, the
  geometric rule decides, as in M12.

### Lines and scripts (D194)

- **Wrapping** (`Builder.wraps`):
  - A line carries on from the one above only when its first word wouldn't have fitted
    after that line, given the widest line in the column.
  - Monospaced text never wraps: three or more lines of six or more characters whose
    width per character agrees within 3%.
  - This applies to the list pass (hanging, paragraph and lowercase carry-on lines) and
    to text inside poster columns.
- **Right to left:** when most letters are Arabic or Hebrew, the boxes are mirrored before
  the passes run, and the structure is mirrored back (a right map becomes a left map,
  `fishbone.left` becomes `fishbone.right`).
- **Vertical CJK:** when most CJK lines of two or more characters come back from Vision as
  columns (1.8× taller than wide), the page is turned so that columns read right to left
  become rows read top to bottom. A one-sided map read that way becomes an org chart
  going down. Other shapes get the canvas's default.
- **Small text:** when the median line is 22 px tall or less, the picture is read again at
  twice the size. At 1×, Vision read 20 px labels backwards ("S1001" for "Tools") and
  dropped one.

### Known gaps

- **Fishbones:**
  - A cause's own sub-causes aren't modelled. Stacked tightly under a cause, they join
    it as wrapped text or read as separate causes. **Fixed 2026-10-22 (D237).**
  - Bones crossing text garble Vision's reading. In the deeper offsite map drawn as a
    fishbone, it read "Workshops" as "WORSTiONS" and ran two labels together, so that
    map isn't on the bench.
  - A fishbone whose bones each have a single cause has no lean to check. It's accepted
    on the other rules, so a one-sided map with an even number of one-child branches
    and nothing on the root's row could pass as one. None of the bench's layouts do.
- **Connector tracing:**
  - It only runs in the map pass. Org charts and timelines go through the column pass,
    and indented trees through the list pass, where the layout already decides.
  - Brace layouts don't trace.
  - A connector that passes within 0.8 of a line of another valid parent's facing side
    stops there.
  - Lines the same colour as the background, or pictures that are 30% ink or more
    (photos, gradients), fall back to the geometry.
  - Not tried on screenshots from other apps: Xmind, MindNode and the like.
  - Vision's contour detection (`VNDetectContoursRequest`) wasn't used. A breadth-first
    walk over our own ink map answers the one question needed (which topic does this
    line reach?) without fitting paths.
- **Wrapping:**
  - A lowercase line under the widest line of its block still joins, unless the block
    is monospaced: widths can't tell it from a wrap.
  - A paragraph that continues across a page break doesn't join, because the page
    margins separate the lines.
- **Scripts:**
  - Hebrew isn't read. Vision on this Mac (macOS 27.2) lists Arabic (`ar-SA`, `ars-SA`)
    among its 33 languages, but not Hebrew or Persian, so the right-to-left handling is
    tested with Arabic only.
  - Drawn at 28–40 pt, vertical Japanese came back as two of three columns. Vision
    missed the title column with its wide character spacing.
  - Vertical text in a map or fishbone gets the canvas's default structure.
  - Connector tracing is off for mirrored and turned pages.
  - Mixed pages (vertical columns with horizontal headings) read as whichever direction
    most lines have.
- **The CLI** still reads pictures with the layout rules only. `nexus` doesn't link
  Apple Intelligence, so Group plain lists applies only in the app (File › Import…,
  drops, ⇧⌘V and now the merge sheet).
- **File › Import…** shows no page progress for a long PDF. It opens the document when
  every page is read. **Fixed 2026-10-22 (D238).**
- **Not tried by hand in the running app:** a multi-page PDF dropped on the canvas or
  added in the merge sheet, and the progress bar. The controller paths are tested.
