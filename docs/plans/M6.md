# M6 · On-device AI (F4 and AI+)

## Plan

**Goal:**
- Ghost suggestions (⌥Space; ⌃↩ accepts one, ⌃⇧↩ accepts all) and the prompt bar
  (⇧⌘Space).
- The AI panel (⇧⌘J): multiple chats, depth, Discussion mode, and a plan-of-changes diff
  before applying.
- Work breakdown and AI to-dos.
- Translate map, data masking, remove image background, make a sticker, and record to
  map.
- Availability states: Apple Intelligence off, model downloading, unsupported.

**Accept when:**
- The canvas and settings match artboards 28, 29 and 52.
- The first ghost appears in under 1.5 s on Apple silicon.
- There are zero outbound connections.
- Every AI change is a single undo.

### Principles (README §3.4: "a quiet collaborator inside the canvas")

- **Nothing is applied silently.** Model output arrives in one of two forms:
  - **ghosts**: dashed teal suggestions laid out as children, which you accept or
    dismiss;
  - **a change plan**: the panel's "Proposed change" diff (`+3 · ~1`), with Preview on
    canvas and "Apply · one undo".

  Accepting or applying is exactly one undo step. AI-created topics carry ✦
  (`node.ai`) until you edit them.
- **Local only.** The default engine uses `SystemLanguageModel.default` (Foundation
  Models), plus Translation, NaturalLanguage, Vision, Image Playground and
  SpeechAnalyzer.
  - Private Cloud Compute is never referenced.
  - The sandbox keeps *no* network entitlement, so the system blocks outbound
    connections. A test checks the entitlements and the sources.
- **Testable seam.** `AIEngine` is a protocol in NexusCore. Its implementations:
  - `AppleEngine` (NexusAI, Foundation Models) is the default;
  - `UnavailableEngine` reports why AI can't run;
  - `ScriptedEngine` is deterministic, for tests and offline previews.

### Model (NexusCore)

- `AIAvailability`: ready, working, `off` (Apple Intelligence turned off),
  `downloading`, `unsupported`. Each has a one-line explanation and actions (Open System
  Settings, Use a local model).
- `MapContext`: an indented outline of the canvas (or a branch) with notes, labels and
  `@` references, trimmed by depth (Quick / Balanced / Deep).
- `ChangePlan`: operations — add a child (under a node or under a planned add), rename,
  set a note, add a marker or label, make a to-do, move.
  - It renders the diff summary and lines ("+ Churn › Win-back email",
    "~ “Churn” → “Retention & churn”").
  - `apply` runs as a single edit and marks new topics `ai`.
- `AIChat` (in the canvas file): title, messages, depth, discussion flag. Each message
  can carry a plan and whether it was applied.
- `AIAction`:
  - Expand branch, Find gaps, Rewrite (shorter), Summarize, Reorganize.
  - Generate map, Import to map, Describe to diagram, Threat suggestions.
  - Explain, Write it up, Diagram as code.
  - Break down, Make to-dos.

  Each declares whether it returns ghosts, a plan or text.

### NexusAI (new target)

- `AppleEngine`:
  - `@Generable` types: `Ideas`, `PlanDraft` (adds, renames, notes), `Breakdown`
    (phases with priority and duration), `TodoList`, `ChatReply` (answer plus optional
    plan).
  - Streaming ghost ideas: each new title becomes a ghost as soon as it arrives.
  - The session is prewarmed when the panel opens or ⌥Space is first pressed.
- `Translator`: Translation framework, topics and labels, optionally notes; writes a new
  canvas.
- `Masker`: NaturalLanguage person names, plus regexes for emails, phones and keys/tokens.
  You review each hit before a masked copy is written.
- `SubjectLift`: Vision foreground mask that removes an image's background. Keep both, or
  replace the original.
- `StickerMaker`: Image Playground `ImageCreator` with Animation, Illustration and Sketch
  styles. The prompt is pre-filled from the topic and branch; the pick lands as the
  topic's sticker with its background removed.
- `Recorder`: SpeechAnalyzer with SpeechTranscriber from the microphone. A live
  transcript feeds the model, which proposes key points as ghosts; the full transcript
  goes into the root's note.

### Canvas and UI (NexusCanvas, app)

- **Ghosts:**
  - Pseudo-children in the scene (laid out with the branch), drawn dashed teal, each
    animating in over 250 ms.
  - ↑↓ browse; ⌃↩ accepts the highlighted ghost; ⌃⇧↩ accepts all; Esc dismisses.
  - Typing ignores ghosts.
  - With "Suggest ideas after a pause" on, ghosts appear on their own.
- **Prompt bar (⇧⌘Space):** anchored to the selection, with a field and chips (Expand
  into a sprint plan, Find gaps, Rewrite shorter, Summarize, Turn this branch into a
  DFD). Results arrive as ghosts or a plan.
- **AI panel (⇧⌘J):**
  - A right sidebar with a chat picker and "+ New chat"; Quick / Balanced / Deep; a
    Discussion-mode toggle.
  - Messages with `@node` chips, and proposed-change cards with Preview on canvas and
    Apply · one undo.
  - Action chips (also in ⌘K) and an input with `@` autocomplete.
  - Footer: "Apple on-device model · Nothing leaves this Mac".
- **Work breakdown:** phases with P1/P2 markers and duration labels, shown as ghosts with
  a "Breaking down… Stop" chip.
- **AI to-dos:** checkable steps under the topic, with a parent progress ring.
- **Translate, Mask, Record:** sheets that follow artboard 52. Remove background and Make
  a sticker are on the image or topic context menu and in ⌘K.
- **Toolbar chip:** "Apple on-device · ready / working / off".
  - When AI can't run, every entry point (⌥Space, prompt bar, palette) shows the inline
    explanation (artboard 29, right).
  - No entry point disappears.
- **Settings › AI (artboard 29):** a Settings window opened with ⌘,. M8 adds the other
  panes.
  - Model: Apple on-device (ready); MLX and local server, shown but disabled (see the
    decisions).
  - Toggles: suggest after a pause, panel depth, Discussion default, Image Playground and
    Translation status, mark AI nodes ✦, status chip; the ghost key display.

### Tests

- `ChangePlan`: apply is one undo; adds under planned adds; renames; the diff text; ✦ is
  cleared when you edit.
- `MapContext` serialisation and depth trimming.
- Ghosts with `ScriptedEngine`:
  - ⌥Space streams ghosts that lay out as children; ↑↓, ⌃↩, ⌃⇧↩ and Esc behave.
  - Each accept is one undo; typing doesn't accept.
  - When unavailable, the explanation shows and nothing changes.
- Panel: chats persist in the file; Discussion mode never produces a plan; Apply is one
  undo; Preview shows the plan as ghosts.
- Actions: break down (markers and labels), to-dos (todo and progress), rewrite (rename
  plan).
- Masking detection (emails, phones, keys, names) and the masked copy; translation of a
  canvas with a scripted translator; subject lift on a synthetic image.
- Privacy:
  - The entitlements have no network keys.
  - No `URLSession`, `NWConnection` or `PrivateCloudCompute` in the sources.
  - AI is never called when it's off.
- Performance: the first streamed ghost arrives in under 1.5 s (live, when Apple
  Intelligence is available).
- Snapshots compared against artboards 28, 29 and 52.

Built:
- **NexusCore AI model:** `AIAvailability`, `MapContext` (outline at Quick / Balanced / Deep, paths, `@` references), `ChangePlan` (adds, including under earlier adds; renames; notes; moves; to-dos; diff lines and counts; JSON), `AIChat` / `AIMessage`, `AIAction` (16 actions, each producing ghosts, a plan or text), `PlanDraft` (resolves the model's topic names to ids), and the `AIEngine` protocol with `ScriptedEngine`.
- **NexusAI (new target):**
  - `AppleEngine`: Foundation Models with guided generation (`IdeaList`, `Breakdown`, `TodoSteps`, `DraftReply`). Ideas stream, so each ghost appears as soon as the next one starts. A prewarmed session waits for the next request. Discussion mode uses its own instructions.
  - `Masker`: emails, keys, phones (regex) and person names (NaturalLanguage).
  - `MapTranslator`: Translation framework, installed pairs only.
  - `SubjectLift`: Vision foreground mask.
  - `StickerMaker`: Image Playground `ImageCreator`.
  - `MapRecorder`: SpeechAnalyzer with `SpeechTranscriber`.
- **Canvas:**
  - Ghosts are scene-only pseudo-nodes laid out as real children. ↑↓ browse, ⌃↩ accepts the highlighted ghost (a phase brings its steps), ⌃⇧↩ accepts all, Esc dismisses, clicking accepts, and typing passes through. Duplicates of existing children are dropped.
  - Prompt bar (⇧⌘Space): chips; conversion phrases ("into a DFD") open Convert on that type.
  - AI panel (⇧⌘J): chats per canvas, depth, Discussion mode, `@` autocomplete, streaming text answers, plan cards with Preview on canvas (the adds appear as ghosts) and Apply, action chips, and Stop. The canvas narrows to make room for the panel.
  - Sheets: Translate, Mask, Remove background (Keep both / Replace original), Make a sticker, Record to map.
  - Availability: every entry point checks first and shows the explanation in place, with Open System Settings and Use a local model. The toolbar chip reads ready / working / off.
- **Settings window (⌘,):** the AI pane, with the other nine panes listed. "Suggest ideas after a pause", "Mark AI-created nodes with ✦" and the status chip are all wired.
- **Format:** `ai/chats.json` (written only when there are chats), with `chats`, `chatMessage`, `changePlan` and `changeOp` added to the schema. The validator checks the file.

Defects found during M6 review and fixed:
- A free-form panel question was sent as a text-only "Explain", so it could never propose a change.
- The canvas didn't make room for the AI panel; the map and ghost hints sat underneath it.
- Overlays anchored inside the canvas view (prompt bar, ghost hints, slash menu) followed the system's dark appearance instead of the canvas theme.
- The panel's leading border was a `Divider` in an overlay, which drew a horizontal line across the middle of the panel.
- The highlighted ghost's ⌃↩ badge had no rect (it was set after measurement) and never drew. It's now a chip beside the ghost, on its outer side.
- Prominent buttons lost their fill in inactive windows. Apply and Open System Settings now use an explicit style.
- **Reflow performance had regressed since M2** (7.8 ms → 43 ms for 1,011 topics; under parallel test load it broke the 50 ms budget). Profiling showed a third of each rebuild went to re-deriving the theme layers and formatting colours as hex for every node. These layers are now built once per style environment: 24 ms.
- The live latency test ran in parallel with the Vision test and read 2.0 s. On-device model tests now run serially.

Known gaps carried forward:
- **Local models:** MLX / Core AI and a localhost server are shown in Settings but disabled (D82).
- **Live-only paths:** translation, stickers and recording need the system's assets (language pairs, Image Playground, speech models) and are checked by hand, not in CI. Their pure parts are tested.
- **Not yet built from artboard 52's footer:** the Brainstorm document type and ✦ Organize, and the Safari clipper (a share extension).
- **Context menus:** Remove background and Make a sticker are reachable from ⌘K and the AI menu. The right-click menu arrives with the context menus in M8.
- **Ghost hints:** in a narrow canvas the hint row can overlap a nearby topic.
