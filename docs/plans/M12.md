# M12 · Image to map

## Plan

**Goal:** Close the gap M7 left ("Images or PDFs via AI") and match Xmind's Image to Mind
Map. A screenshot, photo, poster or PDF page becomes an editable map, entirely on this Mac.
- **No network.** OpenNexus has no network entitlement, and `NetworkPrivacyTests` bans
  networking APIs.
- **Text recognition:** Vision.
- **Hierarchy:** layout rules, with Apple Intelligence as an option.

**Accept when:**
- A PNG, JPEG, HEIC or screenshot, or a PDF's first page, imports as an editable map from:
  - File › Import…;
  - a drop on Home or on empty canvas;
  - a paste.
- The hierarchy is rebuilt from layout for three kinds of picture:
  - an indented list or document;
  - a mind-map screenshot with a central topic;
  - a poster or org chart with columns.
- The result shows the import report. Added to an open document, it's one undo step.
- Tests:
  - the layout logic, on synthetic text boxes for each of the three kinds;
  - real Vision on a picture drawn in the test.

### Reading the text (D150)

- **`ImageText.recognize`** (NexusInterchange) decodes the picture with ImageIO. A PDF's
  first page is rendered at 2,400 px. It runs `VNRecognizeTextRequest` (accurate,
  language correction, automatic language) and returns line boxes in image pixels.
- **`.image` is an `InterchangeFormat`:** png, jpg, jpeg, heic, heif, tif, tiff, gif,
  bmp, webp and pdf. `Interchange.importFile` reads images synchronously, so the CLI
  (`nexus import poster.png`) and the merge sheet take them like any other file.

### Rebuilding the hierarchy (D151)

`ImageOutline.build(_:title:)` is pure: recognised boxes in, an `ICanvas` out.
- **Clean-up:**
  - Marks with no letters or digits, or read with confidence under 0.3, are dropped and
    counted.
  - Pieces of one visual line are joined.
  - A mid-line "•" reads as "·".
- **Title over columns** (posters, org charts):
  - The title is the largest line, with at most one line above it.
  - Columns are gutters no body line crosses, and their lines must share rows. An indented
    list's short parents never share a row with their children.
  - Under the title, each row of side-by-side headings takes the text beneath it, up to
    halfway to the next heading, and the split repeats level by level.
- **A map's centre** (mind-map screenshots):
  - The centre is at least 1.1× the median text size. Most text sits beside it, spread
    above and below it; in a small map, it can be on both sides instead.
  - Candidates rank by the fewest lines stacked directly over or under them, then by size.
    A box can come back too tall when it swallows a connector.
  - Each topic hangs from the cheapest topic between it and the centre: to its left on
    the right side, to its right on the left side, above it below the centre. The cost is
    the gap plus 1.5× the offset across.
  - Branches are ordered clockwise from the top, as a balanced map lays them out. A
    one-sided map reads top to bottom and gets the right or left map structure.
  - A topic's text that wraps onto a second line is joined first.
- **A list** (documents, slides, notes) in reading order:
  - Each line hangs from the nearest earlier line with a more senior key: much larger
    text (1.5× body), then indentation, then larger text (1.25×), then no bullet before
    a bullet (bullet kinds in order of appearance), then fewer number levels ("1.2" is
    under "1.").
  - Wrapped lines join the line above:
    - a hanging line under a bullet;
    - a paragraph's next line;
    - a line that carries on in lowercase.
  - Bullets and numbers are stripped. ☐ and ☑ become to-dos.
- **The root:** a single top-level topic is the central topic. Otherwise one is made from
  the file name.

### Apple Intelligence (D152)

- **Settings › AI › Group plain lists in imported images**, off by default.
- **Used only when the layout shows no nesting** below the main topics, with 4–80 lines.
- **What the model does:** `ImageStructurer.headings` asks for the numbers of the lines
  that head the lines after them, through guided generation. `ImageOutline.grouped`
  then does the grouping in code.
- **When it's rejected:** unless it adds a level with at most half the lines as
  headings, or after 30 seconds. The model never writes or drops text.
- **The report** reads "Image · Apple Intelligence" when the grouping was used.

### Where it lands (D153, D154)

- **File › Import…** (`AppDelegate.importImage`): reads off the main thread and opens a
  new document with the import report. The open panel now lists pictures and PDFs.
- **Home:** dropped files open (`.nexus`) or import (any importable format, pictures
  included).
- **Empty canvas:** a dropped picture goes through `CanvasController.importImage`.
  - It adds a canvas after the current one, as one undo ("Image to Map"), then the
    report.
  - An untouched mind map (only its central topic) is filled in place and keeps its look.
  - A picture dropped on a topic still becomes its image.
- **Edit › Paste Image as Map (⇧⌘V):** the clipboard's picture, PDF or image file, the
  same way. ⌘V with a picture still puts it on the selected topic.
- **The import report** lists what wasn't converted:
  - "Shapes, lines, colours and pictures (text only)";
  - unreadable marks;
  - PDF pages after the first.

## Results

Done. All of the above is built and tested. `swift test`, `swift format lint --strict`
and the Xcode app build pass.

### Accuracy

- **Round trips:** `ImageRoundTripTests` exports two OpenNexus maps as PNG and reads them
  back with real Vision.
  - The maps: the launch plan (5 branches, 11 leaves) and a deeper offsite plan (three
    levels, with a topic that wraps onto two lines).
  - Ten layouts each: balanced, right and left mind maps; right and left logic charts;
    org chart; tree down; indented tree; horizontal timeline; brace.
  - **All 20 outlines come back exact:** same text, same parents, same order.
  - The same through the CLI: `nexus import offsite.png` on the org-chart export, then
    `nexus export` to Markdown, prints the original outline.
- **What the bench caught**, all now fixed:
  - Vision returned a 79 px box for "Channels" (it took in a connector), which won the
    tallest-box test for the centre.
  - Words with descenders read up to 35% taller, which made body text look like headings.
  - A two-line topic read as a title.
  - An org chart's leaf gutters are wider than the gaps between its top-level groups, so a
    flat column split cut it in the wrong places.
  - The box nearest the centre of an org chart passed as a map's centre.
- **Apple Intelligence** (Apple's on-device model, on this Mac):
  - Re-nesting the round-trip outlines with guided generation (line numbers with depths)
    kept only 2 of 10 correct, against 10 of 10 for the layout rules. Each answer took
    about 3 s.
  - Picking headings in five plain lists was fully right on about two. For example, it
    found "Groceries" but not "Hardware", and gave a plain to-do list a heading.
  - So it stays off by default, used only where the layout gives no structure (D152).

### Tests

New this milestone: 15 test functions; the round trip runs 10 cases of two maps each.
- **`ImageOutlineTests`** (8, synthetic boxes):
  - an indented list with a title, bullets and a dropped connector mark;
  - numbered items and checkboxes;
  - a wrapped bullet;
  - a centred mind map;
  - a right map with a wrapped topic;
  - a two-column poster with a footer;
  - grouping plain lists;
  - the import report.
- **`ImageVisionTests`** (2), with real Vision:
  - an indented list drawn in the test;
  - a two-page PDF drawn in the test.
- **`ImageRoundTripTests`** (1 × 10 layouts × 2 maps): as above.
- **`ImageToMapTests`** (3):
  - an untouched canvas filled in place, as one undo;
  - a canvas added next to a map, as one undo;
  - pictures read from the pasteboard.
- **`ImageStructurerTests`** (1): the grouping prompt.

The first Vision request on this Mac took about 35 s while it loaded its models; after
that, a picture reads in 0.2–0.3 s.

### Known gaps

- **Fishbone diagrams** don't come back right. Their text sits along diagonal bones,
  which none of the three passes models.
- **Text only:**
  - relationships, boundaries, summaries, colours, markers, icons and pictures in a
    screenshot aren't recognised;
  - connector lines aren't traced, so in a curved map a branch that sits further out
    than a neighbour's child can hang from that child.
- **Only the first PDF page** is read; the report counts the rest.
- **Reading order is left to right.** Right-to-left scripts and vertical CJK text aren't
  handled. Photos of handwriting or whiteboards weren't tried.
- **Lowercase lines can join:** separate lines that start in lowercase and are tightly
  spaced (shell commands, say) join as one wrapped line.
- **The merge sheet and the CLI:**
  - they read pictures with the layout rules only, without Apple Intelligence;
  - the merge sheet reads on the main thread, which takes 0.2–1 s per picture.
- **Not tried by hand in the running app:** File › Import… with a picture, drops on Home
  and canvas, and ⇧⌘V. They're built, and the controller paths are tested.
- **Apple Intelligence grouping** is opt-in because it's unreliable (D152). A better
  on-device model, or a tuned adapter, could make it the default.
