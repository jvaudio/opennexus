### Fishbone sub-causes (D237)

- **Candidates:** a line tucked under the nearest line above it on its side of the spine
  (across from it, at most 1.2 lines below), with its end nearest the bone half a line or
  more behind that line's; or level with a line above that is a candidate too.
- **The test:** the bones are fitted without the candidates. A candidate is a sub-cause when
  it sits further behind its cause's bone than the bone's causes do: by ¾ of a line, or
  half a line when its text is 0.8× its cause's or smaller. The rest are causes again, and
  the bones are refitted until the sub-causes settle.
- **Nesting:** top down; level with a sub-cause above, a sibling; set back from it, its
  child.
- **Wrapping:** in this fit a tucked line isn't merged into the line above as wrapped text,
  and neither is a line starting with a capital under a sub-cause.
- **Fallback:** used only when it finds sub-causes. Otherwise the plain fit (D192) runs,
  unchanged.

### Import progress (D238)

- **`ImageImportProgress`** (NexusCanvas) runs the reading in a task, keeps pages read and
  pages to read, and gives an outcome once: imported, failed or cancelled. `onManyPages`
  fires when the source turns out to have two or more pages.
- **The app** (`AppDelegate.importImage`) then shows `ImageImportView` in a small panel:
  "Importing “name”", the progress bar, "Reading page N of M…", and Cancel (Esc). Several
  imports cascade their panels.
- **`ReadingProgressBar`**, taken out of the merge sheet's row and used by both: labelled
  "Reading name", the detail as its value, updating frequently. No animation.
- **`ImageText.recognize`** reports (0, pages) before the first page, so the page count
  shows from the start, in the merge sheet and drop toasts too.

### Floating topics in Markdown (D239)

```markdown
<!-- Floating topics -->

- Parking lot
  Ideas for after GA.
  - [ ] Docs site @Kim 2026-11-16→ 10% after:^n13
    - [x] Wiki at \50%
  - [ ] Security audit (P2) @[Ana Lee] →2026-12-18 ^n22
```

- Bullets after the marker are floating topics, up to the next heading or rule.
- Anchors and the Comments appendix include floating topics, so a main-tree task can wait
  on a floating one, and a floating topic's comments come back to it.

### Known gaps

- **OpenNexus's own fishbone layout** stacks a cause's sub-causes under it without making
  room, so they overlap the next cause on the bone (drawn "Staffing" over "New hires"). Its
  exports with sub-causes therefore don't round-trip, and none are on the bench. The layout
  (`Structures.fishbone`) was outside this milestone. Closed in M30 (D240, D241).
- **Sub-causes on a lower bone** are told apart only by their distance from the bone. One
  set back less than ¾ of a line more than the causes (half a line in smaller text) reads
  as a cause. **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D270).**
- **A wrapped sub-cause** whose second line starts with a capital reads as two sub-causes. **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D271).**
- **Sub-causes drawn along their own small bones** (a rib per sub-cause, branching off the
  cause's rib) aren't read as such; only stacked ones are. **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D270).**
- **The import window** appears only for sources of two or more pages. A single picture
  with Group plain lists on can take up to 30 s in the model with no window. **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D272).**
- **Not tried by hand in the running app:** the import window and Cancel.
