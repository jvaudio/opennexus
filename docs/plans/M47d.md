# M47d · Web viewer, full

The last of M47's four parts (`collaboration-and-reach.md`, Phase 5): `web/viewer-app`
grows from M44's share route into a full viewer. It opens a `.nexusjson`, a `.nexus`
folder or a zipped package from its viewer copy, and shows the outline of files without
one. It presents the deck, installs as an offline PWA, embeds in wikis, and has an Open
in OpenNexus button.

Decision #12 (D378) covers the apps; no other row of the plan's "Decisions for Jerry"
table is needed. On Oct 31, 2026 Jerry chose what Open in OpenNexus does with a shared
map: the app opens the link itself (below, and D441).

## What we know before starting

- **The site** (`web/viewer-app`, GitHub Pages) has an about page at its root, the share
  page `/s/` (M44) and the join page `/join/` (M46). Its address is a path on
  `jvaudio.github.io/opennexus/` until decision #4's domain is named, so every URL in it
  is relative. Pages are static, so a `<meta>` CSP is all the policy there is.
- **The viewer** (`web/viewer-core`, M43) draws a bundle: per canvas, the app's SVG in
  two layouts, each topic's frame, the outline and each topic's details. `mount(host,
  bundle, {asset})` builds the whole UI: bar, outline, map and details. It has no
  Present, and no way to hide its chrome. HTML exports embed it
  (`scripts/build-viewer.sh`).
- **Viewer copies** (D314): a package's `render/viewer.json`, and a `render` key in a
  `.nexusjson` only when Settings › Files & Backup › Also in single-file documents is on
  (off by default). A copy names its assets: `assets/<name>` in a package, base64 under
  the `.nexusjson`'s `assets` key. There is never a copy for an encrypted document.
- **The deck** is computed in Swift (`Deck.slides`: generated slides after the user's
  order, merges and skips); the bundle doesn't carry it.
- **Share links** (M44) seal a viewer bundle, not a document, so the app can't open one as
  it was. The app has no network entitlement; `OpenNexusNet.xpc` (Mac) and `NexusNet` in
  the iOS app reach only the hosts of configured destinations. `opennexus://` links on the
  Mac and iOS handle capture, the Inbox, widgets, `new` and `join`.
- **WebAssembly** (D386, M47a): the browser doesn't lay maps out, so there's no live view
  of a co-editing session, and a file without a viewer copy shows only its outline.
- **Tests:** Playwright 1.64.1 runs Chromium and WebKit. Its Firefox doesn't start on
  macOS 27 (D326) and is still the newest release.

## Plan

### The deck in the bundle (Swift)

- Each canvas of a document or canvas scope gains `deck` in its bundle: the slides of
  `Deck.slides` that play (title, kicker, focus topics, highlighted topics, the note when
  notes are included), with the presentation's style and aspect. Branch and zone scopes
  have no deck. `viewer.schema.json` gains it as optional, so version 1 stands.

### viewer-core

- **Present** (a bar button when the canvas has a deck; `P`): full window, the map
  dimmed but for the slide's focus. The camera flies from slide to slide along van Wijk
  and Nuij's path, framing the focus topics' frames from the index at the deck's aspect.
  A caption shows the kicker, title and count. → Space PageDown go on, ← PageUp go back,
  Home End, `N` shows the note, Esc leaves. With reduced motion the camera cuts.
- **Mount options:** `chrome: "none"` (embed: the map, its pan and zoom, and a link out;
  no bar, outline or details) and `actions`, links the host adds to the bar.

### The site (viewer-app)

- **The viewer at the root:** Open File… (`.nexusjson`, `.zip`), Open Folder… (the File
  System Access API's directory picker where there is one, else a folder input), and
  drop of a file or folder anywhere. The about text stays below.
- **Readers:** a `.nexusjson`'s `render` key and its base64 assets; a folder's
  `render/viewer.json` and `assets/`; a zip (stored or deflated entries, read with
  `DecompressionStream`), with or without a top `Name.nexus/` folder. Assets become blob
  URLs through `mount`'s `asset`.
- **Without a viewer copy:** the outline of each canvas from its JSON (topics' text in
  order, with notes), and how to add a copy (the setting, then save again). An encrypted
  document says it opens only in OpenNexus. Anything else says what it isn't.
- **PWA:** `manifest.webmanifest` (the brand's 192 and 512 icons, standalone, scope and
  start at the site's root), and `sw.js`, which precaches the site at build time
  (versioned by its contents' hash) and serves it cache-first. Other requests, such as
  share fetches, go to the network untouched. Every page registers it.
- **Embed:** `?embed` on a share link (`s/?embed#…`) mounts with no chrome and a link to
  the full view, for iframes in wikis.
- **Open in OpenNexus** on the share page's bar: `opennexus://view#<code>`, the link's
  fragment with its key. For a password link, that's the key the password derived, once
  the map is open.

### Open in OpenNexus (the apps; D441)

- `NetJob` gains `read` with the link's source: the service fetches the sealed map from
  the link's own hosts only, which it works out from the link itself (the URL's host, or
  GitHub's API and raw gist host). It follows M44's status rules: 404, 410 or 403 means
  the link is off. The user opening the link is what configures those hosts; nothing else
  is reachable, and every request is logged.
- `ShareCenter.openLink` takes `opennexus://view#…` or a share page's link, checks
  "Use the network" and "Share links" (both off on a fresh install), reads and unseals the
  map. A link without its key (a password link pasted) says to open it in the browser.
- `ViewerBundleImport` (NexusInterchange) turns the bundle into a document: each canvas's
  type, title and outline, with every topic's text, note, comments, link, labels, markers
  and task state, and attachments the link carried. The import report names what a share
  link doesn't carry: styles, connections, images and positions.
- Mac `Integrations.handle` and iOS `TouchScene.handle` open `opennexus://view#…` as a new
  untitled document with its import report, as File › Import… does.

### Tests

- **Swift:** the deck in bundles; the schema; `read` (a URL, a gist, a truncated gist, a
  turned-off link, a redirect to another host refused, only the link's hosts allowed);
  `openLink` (network off, a key link, a password link without its key, expired, a damaged
  link); the import of LaunchPlan's bundle and of a technical diagram; the apps' link
  routing. `NetworkPrivacyTests` gains `read`'s hosts.
- **Fixtures** (`npm run fixtures`): a package with and without its viewer copy, a
  `.nexusjson` with and without `render`, a zip of a package, and an encrypted package.
- **Vitest:** the zip reader, the readers and their errors, the outline fallback, the
  flight's path, the deck in the bundle, and the embed and action options.
- **Playwright** (Chromium and WebKit; Firefox where it starts, D326): open each fixture by
  picker and by drop, compare with the viewer copy, the fallback, Present, offline after
  install, embed, Open in OpenNexus's link, and axe on each.

### Changed from the plan

- The flight takes the app's fixed 0.7 s rather than a duration from the path's length
  (D444), as M42's `FlyPath` does.
- A slide framing the whole map has `focus: []` rather than every topic (D442).
- The service's read job doesn't name its hosts: the service works them out from the link
  (D448), so the app can't widen them.
- Present switches to the other layout when the one showing hides a focus topic (D443),
  which the plan didn't say.

### Defects found

- **`npm run check` failed at lint** since M48: web/math had no tsconfig for typed linting
  (D449).
- **An encrypted package's hint wasn't shown**: the reader looked under `encryption`, and
  the app writes it at the top of the manifest.
- **`baseName("Plan.nexus.zip")` was "Plan.nexus"**.
- **Esc then P ended the new presentation**: the last one's full-screen exit arrived after
  the new one listened for it; Present now ends only after its own full screen was entered
  (D443).
- **In Safari, leaving Present left focus on the page**: a clicked button doesn't take
  focus there; focus goes back to Present.
- **A technical diagram's import reported its tree's edges as connectors** (seven for one
  relationship), since it draws them the same way (D441).

### Known gaps

- Present draws a slide's focus, not the app's Timeline slides (a compact Gantt) or the
  flow step moving along its connector; highlighted topics get a ring instead.
- The flight eases in and out with a quadratic, not the app's Bezier curve.
- Open in OpenNexus shows on every system, including ones without the app; the link then
  does nothing.
- A link's map comes back without styles, themes, positions, relationships, images on
  topics, fields, tree-edge labels ("Yes", "No") or links to other canvases; the import
  report counts what it can see (D441).
- The installed PWA doesn't register as a handler for `.nexusjson` files (out of scope).
- A real folder dragged from Finder, and a frame on another origin, were checked by the
  entry API and a same-origin frame, not by hand (D450).
