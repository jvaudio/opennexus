# M42 · Present, part 4: motion and controls

Builds on M5, M21, M28 and M35 (`starters-guide-present.md`, Feature 3).

## Plan

**Accept when:**

- **A1 · Styles.** Calm, Dynamic (default) and Showtime, deck-wide. Calm reproduces today's
  Present; old decks open unchanged under Calm and keep their slides under Dynamic.
- **A2 · Motion.** Fly-to on a van Wijk–Nuij path (`Motion.fly`); builds per
  `presentStep` (children pop in a stagger apart, connectors draw on); spotlight (dim plus
  blur); Showtime's enlarged, glowing focus. Every value is a `Motion` token, and Reduce
  Motion replaces every effect.
- **A3 · Slides.** Depth (main topics, or every topic with children) and Overview between
  branches; technical decks keep their flow and message order. Slide ids stay stable.
- **A4 · Controls.** Click; B and W; digits then ↩; O; Esc ending on the slide's topic;
  hold ⇧ for a laser; all in the ? list. ⌥⌘P, Present from Selected Topic.
- **A5 · Presenter view.** Full screen on the second display; the current and next slides
  drawn for real; notes in larger type; elapsed time and a clock.
- **A6 · Exports.** Investigate vector PDF; speaker notes in .pptx.
- **A7 · Performance.** Fly-to with motion on: median main-thread frame under 8.3 ms on
  1,000 topics and 16.7 ms on 5,000.
- **A8 · Quality.** `swift test` and `scripts/lint.sh` pass, and the app builds.

### Known gaps

- **Not driven by hand in the running app:** the laser, a real mouse click, the second
  display, and the look of the flight and builds. The model and view are tested headless.
- **No pixel goldens of each style:** reviewed as env-gated renders (`NEXUS_SNAPSHOT_DIR`),
  as the other chrome is.
- **The .pptx notes weren't opened in PowerPoint or Keynote;** the test checks the parts
  and their relationships.
- **Exported slides show no blur:** the exporter draws layers without their Core Image
  filters, so Dynamic's spotlight exports as plain dimming.
