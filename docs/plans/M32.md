# M32 · Xmind timeline and fold control placement

## Plan

**Goal (closing M23's timeline gap and M24's placement gap):** Xmind's horizontal
timeline alternates each event's branch above and below the axis, each growing to the
right as a logic chart; OpenNexus's hangs every event's details below the axis as an
indented list, so Xmind timelines imported looking different. And the mouse's fold
control sat on the topic's branch side even where its lines leave from somewhere else
(a timeline event's details hang from its bottom-left).

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Xmind's timeline:** a new structure lays events on the axis and alternates
  their branches above and below it, each a logic chart growing away from the root, with
  no two topics overlapping and no line crossing a topic, on either side of a mind map.
  Our own horizontal timeline is unchanged.
- **A2 · Choosable:** it has a name, and the Format panel's picker and the right-click
  Structure submenu offer it. Lines, hit testing, drop targets and folding work in it;
  it's self-contained like the other timelines.
- **A3 · Xmind files:** Xmind's `org.xmind.ui.timeline.horizontal` imports as the new
  structure (.xmind and XMind 8, sheet and branch) and exports back exactly; our own
  timeline still exports as Xmind's timeline, reported as an approximation.
- **A4 · Fold control:** in every structure, the "−" and the "+N" sit on the junction
  where the topic's branch line leaves it, derived from the line, and a folded topic's
  "+N" is where its "−" was.

### Known gaps

- **A folded org-chart topic whose children are all leaves** shows its "+N" at the
  middle of its bottom; unfolded, they stack from its bottom-left and the "−" is there.
  The layout can't see whether hidden children are leaves.
- **A fishbone bone's "−"** can overlap the corner of the cause nearest the label.
- **A folded cause's "+N" on a lower fishbone bone** sits by the cause, not where its
  sub-causes' line moves out to clear the bone (D240).
- **Topics with a structure of their own inside a timeline branch** are laid out by the
  timeline (as in every self-contained structure); folded, their control follows their
  own structure's family.
- **The axis runs behind the events** as in our horizontal timeline, so on a nested
  timeline with tinted subtopic fills it shows through them.
- **Our horizontal timeline doesn't survive an Xmind round trip**: it comes back
  alternating (reported on export).
- **Not tried by hand in the running app;** checked through the mouse handlers in tests
  and on offscreen renders.
