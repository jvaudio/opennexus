# M47c · iPhone

The third of M47's four parts (`collaboration-and-reach.md`, Phase 5): the iPad app of
M47b at compact width, on the iPhone. The plan asks for the outline first with a canvas
toggle, a bottom toolbar on the canvas (add child, add sibling, style, more), capture to
the Inbox from widgets and the Share sheet, reading and commenting, presenting over
AirPlay, and technical diagrams that are editable but tuned for viewing.

Decision #12 (iOS/iPadOS 27, TestFlight then the App Store) was confirmed on Oct 29, 2026
(D378). No other row of the plan's "Decisions for Jerry" table is needed by M47c.

## What we know before starting

- The iPad app (M47b, `dd0104a`) is one SwiftUI scene per window: `TouchSceneView` shows
  Home or a `TouchDocumentScreen`. The project targets the iPad only
  (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY` 2), and its scheme is "OpenNexus iPad".
- The document screen is built for a wide window: a canvas tab strip, a navigation bar
  with a 240-point view-mode picker and five buttons, the Format panel as an
  `.inspector`, the AI panel beside the canvas, and request popovers fixed to the Mac's
  sizes (`presentationCompactAdaptation(.popover)`).
- Home has a 230-point sidebar and a four-column grid; Settings has a sidebar of panes
  beside each pane; the starter picker asks for 700 × 560 points. None fits a phone.
- `TouchOutlineView` draws the Mac's outline in UIKit with margins of 56 points and an
  indent of 32; it shows notes, links and folded counts, not comments.
- Comments (M14) are a thread on a topic, edited in `CommentsPopover`.
- Touch on the canvas (D391): a one-finger drag on a topic moves it and on empty canvas
  draws a band; two fingers pan. `CanvasPointer` can also pan on an empty drag.
- The Share extension captures to the Inbox through the App Group (M47b). The Inbox
  widget opens the Inbox map; nothing on iOS captures an idea without opening a map.
- Presenting with a second display moves the canvas to an external display scene and
  shows the presenter view (D398). On the iPhone, AirPlay's Screen Mirroring gives the app
  that scene. `ExternalDisplay` is the seam a test can drive.
- iPhone simulators on iOS 27: iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 17e, Air, 18 Pro, 18 Pro
  Max, Duo. An iPhone 15 Pro Max is paired with this Mac, but running on it needs the app
  signed with a development team, which this project doesn't set; the numbers are the
  simulator's.

## Plan

### One app for iPad and iPhone

- `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY` 1,2 for the app and its four extensions; portrait and both
  landscapes on the iPhone. The scheme becomes "OpenNexus iOS"; `scripts/test-ipad.sh`
  follows. Usage descriptions stop naming the iPad.
- **The layout follows the horizontal size class, not the device:** compact width gets
  the iPhone's layout, so an iPad in Slide Over or a narrow split gets it too.

### The document at compact width

- **Outline first:** a map opens in the Outline; the navigation bar's middle is an
  Outline | Canvas toggle. Split isn't offered (a Split in effect becomes the Outline);
  Gantt is under More. Technical diagrams open in the canvas, fitted to the screen.
- **Canvases:** the title is a menu of the document's canvases with New Canvas and the
  tab menu's actions, in place of the tab strip.
- **The bottom toolbar** (in the Outline and on the canvas): Add Child, Add Sibling,
  Style (the Format panel as a sheet with a medium and a large detent) and More (a menu:
  Comments, Note, Focus, ZEN, Present, Find, Filter, AI, Gantt, Export, Share Link, Print,
  Import, Collaborate, Settings and the rest of the iPad's share menu). The navigation bar
  keeps Documents and the toggle.
- **Sheets for what floats beside the canvas on the iPad:** request popovers (note,
  comments, link, labels, markers, stickers, legend) become sheets sized by detents; the
  AI panel becomes a sheet.
- **The outline at compact width:** narrower margins and indent, and the central topic's
  title a size smaller, so a third level keeps most of the width.

### Reading and commenting

- The outline shows each topic's comments ("2 comments · Ana: …"); tapping that line
  opens the thread. The canvas's comment badge does the same.
- The thread is the shared `CommentsPopover` in a sheet; the author is Settings ›
  Editing › Comment author, or the device's name.

### Technical diagrams, tuned for viewing

- They open in the canvas fitted to the screen and refit when the phone turns.
- At compact width a one-finger drag pans the canvas unless it starts on a selected
  topic, which it moves; a long press still opens the menu, and the bottom toolbar adds
  steps. On the iPad nothing changes.

### Capture

- **Quick Capture on iOS:** `opennexus://inbox/add` opens a capture sheet (the Mac's
  `QuickCaptureModel`, with a touch view): one field, the target map (Inbox or a recent
  map), Save. Ideas go through `CaptureComposer` as Share captures do, and Home stays
  where it was.
- **Widgets:** the Inbox widget gets a capture button (the small one is all capture link
  but its count), and a Lock Screen widget (circular and rectangular) opens Quick
  Capture.
- **The Share extension** runs on the iPhone as on the iPad.

### Presenting

- On the iPhone, Present fills the screen; a tap or a swipe left advances, a swipe right
  goes back.
- With Screen Mirroring to an Apple TV or a Mac (AirPlay), the slides go to the external
  scene and the iPhone shows the presenter view, as on the iPad (D398). When presenting
  starts on an iPhone with no second display, a toast says Screen Mirroring sends it to a
  TV.

### Home, Settings and the starter picker at compact width

- **Home:** no sidebar; Home, Recents, Templates and the Inbox are a picker under the
  greeting, the New cards and recents are two columns, the shelf scrolls sideways.
- **Settings:** a list of panes that pushes each pane, with search above.
- **Starter picker:** full screen, its categories a scrolling row and its cards in two
  columns.

### Tests

- **Hosted tests on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator:** the whole hosted suite (goldens, text
  parity, timing, touch, keys, integration, network, live) runs there as on the iPad, and
  a new `CompactTests`: a map opens in the Outline and a technical diagram in the canvas,
  fitted; the toggle; the bottom bar's actions; the canvas menu; comments from the
  outline and the canvas; a drag that pans and one that moves; Quick Capture from its
  link to the Inbox; Present's swipes; the presenter view on the phone with the canvas on
  a simulated external display.
- **UI tests on the iPhone:** the F1 map built by touch only in the Outline; Quick
  Capture from the widget's link to the Inbox, then the idea edited in the Outline;
  Present; a comment; `performAccessibilityAudit` on Home, the Outline, the canvas and
  Settings, at the default text size and Dynamic Type's largest.
- **By hand in the simulator:** the widgets on the home screen and the Lock Screen, the
  Share sheet from Safari, landscape.
- **iPad and Mac:** both suites still pass on the iPad; `swift test`, the goldens and
  `RenderDump` unchanged.

### Changed from the plan

- **The toggle sits at the navigation bar's trailing end and the canvas menu is the
  title,** rather than the toggle in the middle: the title is where iOS puts a document's
  own menu, and the bar has room for three items (D415).
- **Home's recents are a list,** not two columns: a list row has the width for a map's
  name and date. The New cards are two columns, as planned (a sideways row, tried first,
  left cards cut by the screen's edge, which the audit failed).
- **The small Inbox widget still opens the Inbox;** the medium one has a Capture tile
  beside its three newest ideas, and the Lock Screen widget has all three accessory
  families (circular, rectangular, inline). A small widget takes one tap target, so it
  can't be "all capture link but its count".
- **The keyboard carries Add Child, Add Sibling and Done on the iPhone** (D416): the plan
  put them only in the bottom toolbar, which the keyboard covers.
- **Some of it reaches the iPad too:** the Outline's comments line, Present's swipes and
  the shared share menu items; the bottom toolbar also shows in the Outline.
- **The presenter view is checked in the UI test with a simulated display**
  (`-uitest-display`), and the canvas's move to the external scene in a hosted test with a
  second window standing in for it.

### Defects found

- **The canvas laid its overlay out 545 points wide on a 402-point screen,** clipping the
  chrome at both sides: the status bar's key hints carry a 230-point trailing pad for the
  iPad. At compact width the hints are left out (D415).
- **A fitted flowchart's last steps sat under the bottom toolbar:** the fit used the whole
  view. It now leaves the chrome's room (`fitInsets`, D414).
- **In the iPhone's Outline, a new topic's text went to the wrong topic** (found by the F1
  UI test): the canvas, off screen, took the keyboard after the Outline showed, which
  moved focus off the Outline, so the next topic's edit opened in the canvas's hidden
  editor while the Outline's editor kept the previous text ("Launch planTopic 1"). The
  canvas now leaves the focus alone when it can't take the keyboard
  (`TouchCanvasView.becomeFirstResponder`); `CompactTests.theKeyboardsBarAddsTopicsInTheOutline`
  covers the flow.
- **The iPhone's keyboard had no way to add a topic:** the iPad's shortcuts bar doesn't
  exist there, and the keyboard covers the bottom toolbar (D416).
- **Xcode built `NexusTouch` against a stale `NexusCanvas` module** ("cannot find type
  'QuickCaptureModel'") after the model moved out of the Mac-only file, as in M47b; a clean
  build fixes it.
- **The Outline's notes, comments and links lines weren't accessibility elements** (found
  by the comment UI test): VoiceOver read the topics only, so a comment shown under a
  topic couldn't be reached. Every line is an element now, the comments line a button
  that opens the thread (D416).
- **The presenter view failed the audit:** End's red on grey was under 4.5:1, the clock,
  the timer and a long slide title clipped at larger text, and the slide pictures were
  unlabelled images. End is a plain button, the clock and timer have captions and sit
  side by side or stacked (`TitleAndControl`), titles wrap, and each slide picture is one
  labelled image.
- **The document's title didn't follow Dynamic Type:** it was a custom view in the
  navigation bar. It is the system's title with a title menu now (D415).
- **A flowchart fitted in landscape drew its steps 9 points tall,** under the audit's hit
  area and a finger's: a topic smaller than 44 points now has a 44-point target (D416).
- **Home's New cards ran off the screen's edge** in a sideways row, and iOS's floating
  search field covered the last row and the shelf; both failed the audit's contrast and
  Dynamic Type checks (D418).
- **Cards in a lazy grid failed the audit's Dynamic Type check, on the iPad too:** the
  grid makes a cell anew as the text grows, so the audit loses it. The iPad's Home audit,
  which passed in M47b, failed today on the shelf's second row with an M47b build too, so
  something in the simulator's state changed (what, isn't known). Home's New cards and shelf and
  Shortcuts' profile cards use `ColumnsLayout`, which lays them out at once (D418).
  `ViewThatFits` did the same to the About card's links (it swaps one set of views for
  another); they wrap in a `FlowLayout`, which now gives a child wider than the row the
  row's width.
- **At the largest text size,** Shortcuts' search placeholder and its profile cards
  clipped, and the AI pane's model status squeezed the model's name into a narrow column;
  the placeholder is shorter on iOS, the cards keep their text's height, and the status
  goes under the description on a phone. The About card's links stack when they don't fit.
  Home's search prompt, "Search maps and nodes", clipped in the navigation bar's drawer;
  it is "Search" on a phone.
- **The audit failed the Whiteboard card's contrast,** though its text measures 21:1: the
  card's name was an element of its own that took the whole card's frame, so the audit
  measured it across the picture. The button's label already reads the card ("New
  Whiteboard"), so the name is hidden from accessibility (D418).
- **The audit's own rules, at a phone's size** (`OpenNexusiOSUITests.audit`): Home scrolls,
  so it's audited a screenful at a time as Settings is; a chip cut by the screen's side in
  Home's sideways row is exempt from contrast and clipping as a row cut by the bar is (it's
  checked where it shows whole); clipping has a pass of its own, since its reflow left
  issues with no element when run with the others; and a glyph up to 22 points wide (a
  Settings pane's on its tile) is measured on its strokes (D408).
- **The medium Inbox widget overflowed on the home screen** (found by hand): five ideas
  under a Capture header needed 181 points in a widget about 163 tall on the iPhone 17 Pro,
  so the last idea and the "more" line were cut. It now shows three ideas beside a Capture
  tile, 126 points of content, the small widget's height.
- **The iPhone said "iPad"** (found by hand, clipping a page from Safari): the clipper's
  "Summarised on this iPad", and the same in the clear-chats prompt, the recording note,
  Home's "On My iPad" location and Settings' "iPadOS" section. Each names the iPhone now.
- **The clipper's form sat in the middle of an empty full-height sheet** on the iPhone,
  laid out for the iPad's small form sheet; it keeps to the top now.

### Known gaps

- **No physical iPhone:** every number is the simulator's (out of scope: it needs a signing
  team), and the widgets, the Share sheet and the keyboard's bar ran only in the simulator.
- **Real AirPlay is untried** (A6): the external display is simulated, by a second window
  in the hosted test and by `-uitest-display` in the UI test. The simulator's external
  screen needs a simulator window's I/O menu, and Device Hub wasn't granted (as in M47b).
- **XCUITest taps the keyboard's bar by its coordinates:** the keyboard's window covers the
  screen above the bar's, so XCUITest finds no hit point on its buttons.
- **The iPhone's F1 UI test is slow and, under load, unreliable:** 290 to 450 seconds
  alone, as busy as the Mac is, and 420 with another suite running, since XCUITest types
  each key and waits for the app to idle. Twice, with another device's suite running, it
  lost track of the keyboard's bar (once on a build before the last changes); alone it
  passes, on the final build too. Its count check now lists the Outline's rows when it
  fails.
- **Home's chips past the screen's side aren't audited for contrast:** the audit scrolls
  Home down, not sideways, and a chip the side cuts is exempt; the chips on screen, in the
  same style, are audited.
- **Some Settings text still names the Mac on iOS,** as on the iPad since M47b: "Local
  server on this Mac", "Leave empty to keep this Mac's key", "http:// works only for this
  Mac".
- **The Mac's medium Inbox widget overflows the same way** (178 points of content in
  about 138): M47c leaves the Mac as it is, so it keeps five ideas under a Capture header.
- **Quick Capture's Save can race the simulator's typing:** a save tapped while
  `simctl`'s synthetic keys were still arriving kept part of the text ("Widget c"). A
  person can't type and tap at once, so this is noted, not fixed.
