# M24 · Fold from the mouse

## Plan

**Goal (Xmind parity, found while testing by hand):** Xmind shows a "−" where a topic's
branch lines leave it; clicking it hides that topic's children. OpenNexus folded only
from the keyboard (⌘/, Z) and the right-click menu, and its "+N" badge on a folded
branch couldn't be clicked.

**Accept when:**
- **Hover:** a topic with visible children shows a "−" on its branch side, just past its
  edge, over the branch line. It stays while the pointer moves onto it and goes when the
  pointer leaves the topic or the canvas.
- **Fold:** clicking the "−" folds the branch in one undo step, and the "+N" badge takes
  its place. A selection inside the branch moves to the folded topic.
- **Unfold:** clicking a folded branch's "+N" unfolds it, with no hover needed.
- **Unchanged:** clicking the topic itself still selects it. There's no control on the
  central topic or on leaves, and none outside Select mode, while presenting, in Map
  Shot or in exports.

## Results (2026-10-22)

| | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Hover | **Met.** `NodeLayer.foldButton`: a 16 pt circle in the branch colour over the canvas colour, placed on the topic's branch side (right, left or below), where the "+N" badge goes. |
| Fold | **Met.** `CanvasView.toggleFoldFromControl` uses the same `toggleFold` as ⌘/: one undo step named Fold. |
| Unfold | **Met.** Every folded topic's badge is a click target (2 pt larger than drawn; the "−" is 4 pt larger). |
| Unchanged | **Met.** Covered by tests. The pointing-hand cursor shows only over a control, and the canvas sets no other cursors. |

### Tests

`FoldControlTests` (5):
- `hoverShowsTheMinusOnlyWhereThereAreChildren`
- `theMinusStaysWhileThePointerMovesOntoIt`
- `clickingTheMinusFoldsAndThePlusUnfolds`
- `clickingTheTopicItselfStillSelects`
- `noControlOutsideSelectMode`

These drive the real `mouseMoved`, `mouseDown` and `mouseExited` handlers.

### Known gaps

- The control sits where the "+N" badge does, on the topic's branch side. Where
  children leave from another point (a timeline event's details hang from its
  bottom-left), it isn't on the line itself.
- It's for the mouse only. VoiceOver and the keyboard already fold: the topic's Fold
  action, ⌘/ and Z.
- There's no setting to hide it; Xmind has none either.
