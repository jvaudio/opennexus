# M26 · Context menu gaps

## Plan

**Goal (closing M17's known gaps):**
- **Paste on empty canvas** places the clipboard's topics as floating topics where the
  menu opened, as Xmind does. ⌘V keeps pasting under the selected topic.
- **Zone on empty canvas** makes the zone where the menu opened. ⌥⌘Z keeps wrapping the
  selected floating topics, or going below the map.
- **Format Panel** toggles from a context menu, as ⌘I does: checked while the panel is
  open, and choosing it again closes it. M17 described this as behaviour; check it.
- **The outline and the Gantt get context menus,** built from `CanvasMenuEntry`,
  `MenuBuilder` and the command registry, so items match the canvas's topic menu where
  that makes sense, plus the Gantt's task commands. Their keyboard paths don't change.

(The Format panel's structure-picker naming, also on M17's list, was fixed in D216.)

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Paste at the pointer:** the empty-canvas menu's Paste puts copied topics, pasted
  text and copied zones at the clicked point, in one undo step; ⌘V is unchanged.
- **A2 · Zone at the pointer:** the empty-canvas menu's Zone makes an empty zone at the
  clicked point and opens its title; ⌥⌘Z is unchanged.
- **A3 · Format Panel:** its item is checked while the panel is open and closes it when
  chosen again, from the canvas's and the outline's menus.
- **A4 · Outline and Gantt menus:** right-click and Control-click on a row select it and
  open a menu of registry commands that act on it; the Gantt's empty area has its own
  items. Running an item equals its key, and the views' keys are unchanged.
- **A5 · Quality bars:** strings are localised, every item is labelled, VoiceOver reaches
  the menus with Show Menu, and lint, tests and the app build are clean.

### Pointer placement (D222, D223)

- `CanvasMenuAction` gains `.pasteHere(point)` and `.zoneHere(point)`, next to M17's
  `.floatingTopic(point)`. The items keep Paste's and Zone's titles and keys, show only
  where the command can run, and tell the tour coach they ran.
- `CanvasController.paste(at:)` reads the clipboard as `paste()` does. Topics land as
  floating branches: the first at the point, the others stacked 24 pt apart below it,
  from one layout pass inside the same edit. A zone lands with its corner at the point,
  one line of text as one floating topic, and an image goes on the selected topic.
- `CanvasController.addZone(at:)` adds an empty 420 × 240 pt zone with its top-left
  corner at the point and opens its title.
- Both live in `CanvasController+Pointer.swift`, so `paste()`, `addZone()` and M20's
  zone clipboard are untouched.

### Row menus (D225, D226)

- **Outline row:** the topic menu without the canvas-only items (styles, structures,
  callouts, boundaries, summaries, relationships, popovers), plus Indent, Outdent, Move
  Up and Move Down, each shown only where it can move. Several rows: Cut, Copy, To-Do,
  Format Panel, Delete.
- **Gantt row:** Edit Task…, Mark Done, Progress ▸, Schedule from Today or Reschedule ▸,
  Link Tasks, Remove Dependencies, Cut, Copy, Back to Canvas, Delete. Below the rows: Go
  to Today, Export Gantt…, Back to Canvas.
- `ContextMenus.make` (moved out of `CanvasView`) turns entries into an `NSMenu` for any
  `ContextMenuTarget`: the canvas, `OutlineDocumentView` and `GanttSurface`.
- The outline runs only the items its own menu targets at it. Menu-bar items that reach
  it go up the responder chain as before, so Tab still indents.
- Rows are `MenuRowElement`s: Show Menu opens the row's menu. Show Menu on the view opens
  the selected row's.

### Built

- **NexusCanvas:**
  - `CanvasMenu.swift`: `.pasteHere` and `.zoneHere`, `MenuBuilder.placed`, the outline and
    Gantt menus, `prepareRowMenu`, and the outline's movement rules in `canRun`.
  - `CanvasController+Pointer.swift`: `paste(at:)` and `addZone(at:)`.
  - `ContextMenuViews.swift`: `ContextMenuTarget`, `ContextMenus` (the `NSMenu` factory,

  - `CanvasView.swift`: `makeMenu` and `performMenuAction` use `ContextMenus`.

### Known gaps

- **An image pasted from the empty-canvas menu goes on the selected topic,** as ⌘V puts
  it, not on a new floating topic at the pointer.
- **Outline rows have no custom actions from their menu** (the canvas's topics have
  them, D179). VoiceOver reaches the items through Show Menu.
- **The outline menu leaves out the popover items** (Note, Hyperlink…, Label, Markers…,
  Comment…, Image…): their popovers anchor on the canvas, which the Outline view hides.
- **README §11** has no M26 entry; the coordinator adds it.
