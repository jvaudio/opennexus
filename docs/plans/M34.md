# M34 · Zone selection, part 3

## Plan

**Goal (closing M27's known gaps):** several selected zones could be copied, cut,
duplicated, deleted and dragged together (D228), but a rubber band never selected zones,
Arrange and the Format panel acted on the clicked zone alone, VoiceOver read the topic
under a zone selection, and Duplicate Branch (⌘D on a topic) left callouts behind.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Rubber band:** a band dragged around whole zones selects them (⇧ adds to the
  selected zones), and a band that covers a zone's topics but not the whole zone still
  selects those topics. The choice is recorded.
- **A2 · Arrange:** Bring to Front, Send to Back and the Format panel's Order move every
  selected zone, keeping their order among themselves, one undo step.
- **A3 · Format panel:** the zone section edits every selected zone in one undo step,
  showing values they don't share as mixed.
- **A4 · VoiceOver:** a zone element reads its place among the selected zones ("Ideas.
  Zone around 2 topics. Selected, 1 of 2 selected zones."), with custom actions Add to
  Selection and Remove from Selection, and a Zones rotor.
- **A5 · Duplicate Branch:** ⌘D on a topic carries its callouts (nested ones too) and
  whatever else `graft` carries since D229, with ids remapped, one undo step.

### Approach

- **Band (D258):** `CanvasView.bandZones` lists the zones a band wholly encloses; on
  release they're selected with `selectZones` (with ⇧, after the zones selected when the
  band began, kept in the `rubber` drag). ⌘ skips it, so the band selects topics.
- **Arrange (D259):** `CanvasEditor.reorderZones(ids, order)`; `reorderSelection` and the
  panel's Order call `CanvasController.reorderZones(selectedZones…)`.
- **Format panel (D260):** `ZoneSection` edits `controller.zones(with:)` through
  `updateZones` (one `perform`); `common` / `mixed` read `commonZoneValue`. `PanelToggle`
  takes `mixed`; `PointField` shows "Mixed" as its placeholder. Reset style, Delete and
  Remove zones act on the group.
- **VoiceOver (D262):** `ZoneItem` in `CanvasAccessibility` (children after the topics,
  the Zones rotor); `zoneLabel` reads the selection when asked. `updateAccessibility`
  moves focus to a selected zone; `runMenu` keeps the group for a zone in it.
- **Duplicate (D261):** `CanvasEditor.duplicate` grafts the branch from the canvas itself
  with `sameCanvas: true`, after the original.

### Built

- **NexusCore:** `CanvasEditor.reorderZones`; `duplicate` through `graft`.
- **NexusCanvas:** band selection of zones (`bandZones`, the `rubber` drag's zones);
  `CanvasController.reorderZones`, `updateZones`, `commonZoneValue`, `zones(with:)`;
  group-aware `reorderSelection` and zone Reset style; `ZoneSection` for several zones,
  `PanelToggle(mixed:)`, the panel's Delete through `deleteElement`; `ZoneItem`,
  `zoneItems`, `zoneLabel`, the Zones rotor, zone focus in `updateAccessibility` and the
  canvas's value.
- New strings: Mixed, %lld Zone(s), Remove zones (keep content), Remove Zones, Zones,
  Add to Selection, Remove from Selection, %@. Zone around %@., Collapsed., Selected.,
  Selected, %lld of %lld selected zones.

### Known gaps

- **No live preview of a band's zones:** zones a band will select aren't drawn selected
  until the mouse is released.
- **Export Zone… and Print Zone… in the panel** act on the selected zone only; the export
  sheet's Zones picker exports several.
- **Duplicate Branch drops relationships to topics outside the branch,** as copy and
  paste do (D261); before M34 it copied none at all.
- **Not tried by hand with VoiceOver running;** the zone elements, labels, actions and
  rotor are checked in process.
