# M35 · Present, part 3

## Plan

**Goal (closing M28's known gaps and M21's last one):** the corner legend stays legible
over whatever passes under it, a Timeline slide's chart no longer covers its topics, the
progress dots never meet the legend, and the Present strip's controls are reachable from
the keyboard and labelled for VoiceOver.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Under the legend:** what a slide frames (topics, the outlines framed with them,
  the branch lines between them) is kept clear of the legend; anything else is covered by
  an opaque card, never drawn through it. On screen and in exports.
- **A2 · Timeline charts:** a Timeline slide's topics are framed below the chart, inside
  the stage, and the chart sits under the title, on screen (with and without the strip)
  and in exports, for diagram and branch Timelines. The test fails on M28's framing.
- **A3 · Dots and legend:** they never overlap, from 800 to 2,400 pt wide and decks of 1
  to 150 slides, on screen; exports have no dots.
- **A4 · The strip from the keyboard:** ⇥ / ⇧⇥ reach every strip control, ↩ or Space use
  it, Esc gives the keys back, and L and T still work; each control has a VoiceOver label.
- **A5 · Quality bars:** new strings through Loc (catalog regenerated), no new animation,
  no network APIs, no package dependencies, and `.nexus` files are unchanged.

### Under the legend: a card, and the slide's own drawing kept clear (D263)

- **Choice:** the legend is an opaque card in the slide's background colour, and the
  framing check covers everything the slide frames. Keeping *everything drawn* clear was
  the alternative, but a branch slide's dimmed context runs past the stage on every side,
  so it could only be cleared by zooming out of the slide's own framing.
- **What counts as framed:** the slide's topics, boundaries they touch, zones they're in
  (now part of the framed content, as boundaries were), and the branch lines between
  framed topics, tested as stroked paths.

### Timeline slides frame below the chart (D264)

- `TimelinePlacement` is the chart's one rectangle for the overlay, the framing and the
  export: under the measured title, across the slide, at most 60% of the height to the
  bottom margin. `SlideFit` fits the topics to the stage below it, 24 pt clear.
- On screen the chart is placed at that rectangle instead of flowing after the title, so
  the framing knows where it is before anything is drawn.

### Dots beside the legend (D265)

- `PresentProgress` lays the dots out in the room right of the legend, 24 pt clear: full,
  condensed, then "Slide 12 of 60". The on-screen legend never takes the counter's room.

### The strip from the keyboard (D266)

- `stripControls` lists the strip's controls in ⇥ order; the strip draws from it and
  `activateStripControl` runs one, so a click, ↩ and VoiceOver do the same thing.
- `PresentState.stripFocus` holds the control ⇥ reached; the strip rings it.

### Known gaps

- **The title slide's topmost topic can still touch the title** when the map is tall:
  only Timeline slides frame below something at the top.
- **Relationships between framed topics aren't tested against the legend**, only branch
  lines; a relationship's curve passes under the card like context (D263).
- **⇥ focus is the app's own,** not AppKit's key-view loop: VoiceOver hears the control
  ⇥ reaches, but its cursor doesn't move to it.
- **A long chart gets small rows:** the 60% cap scales a Timeline of many tasks down to
  leave the map room.
- **Not tried by hand in the running app;** checked on harness snapshots and exports,
  which draw the same overlay and slides.
