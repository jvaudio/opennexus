# M33 · Menu and outline gaps

## Plan

**Goal (closing M26's known gaps):** an image pasted from the empty-canvas menu went on
the selected topic, the outline's menu left out the popover items because their popovers
anchored on the hidden canvas, and outline rows had no VoiceOver custom actions. While
there, `nexus validate` said "1 canvases, 1 topics".

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Image at the pointer:** the empty-canvas menu's Paste with an image on the
  clipboard makes a new floating topic holding it at the clicked point, selected, in one
  undo step; ⌘V still puts it on the selected topic.
- **A2 · Popovers from the outline:** the outline's row menu offers Note, Hyperlink…,
  Image…, Label, Markers…, Sticker… and Comment… as the canvas's Insert does, and their
  popovers open on the row while the outline is in use (focused, or the canvas hidden);
  the canvas in front keeps them on the topic.
- **A3 · VoiceOver on outline rows:** each row lists its menu as custom actions, with the
  canvas topic's names for the ones it shares, and each runs on its own row.
- **A4 · CLI counts:** `nexus validate` prints "1 canvas, 1 topic" and "2 canvases, 20
  topics".
- **A5 · Quality bars:** no new strings (the catalog regenerates unchanged), lint, tests
  and the app build are clean.

### Built

- **NexusCanvas:**
  - `CanvasController+Pointer.swift`: `paste(at:)` pastes an image as a floating topic
    (`pasteImage(at:)`); `CanvasController+Elements.swift`: `imageAsset` (shared with
    `setImage`).
  - `CanvasPopovers.swift`: `anchor(for:)` (outline row or canvas topic), the `present`
    seam, focus back to the view the popover hung from. `CanvasController.outlineAnchor`.
  - `OutlineView.swift`: `popoverAnchor`, `rowActions`, `runRowAction`.
  - `CanvasMenu.swift`: `MenuBuilder.insertItems` (shared by the canvas's and the
    outline's Insert ▸), the outline's Insert and multi-selection Label and Markers…,
    `outlineContextMenu(previewing:)`.
  - `ContextMenuViews.swift`: `ContextMenus.run` and `customActions` (the canvas's
    `menuActions` uses it too), `MenuRowElement.customActions`.
- **NexusAgent:** `NexusCLI.counted` and `contentSummary`.
