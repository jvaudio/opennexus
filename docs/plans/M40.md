# M40 · Starter picker

The picker on every + (`starters-guide-present.md`, Feature 1), on M39's catalogue.

## Plan

**Goal:** a new canvas or document starts from any of the 41 starters (16 Blank types and
25 templates) in a picker that is as fast as ⌘T ↩.

**Accept when:**

- **A1 · Entry points.** ⌘T, the tab bar's + and the tab menu's New Canvas open the picker
  as a sheet, and Create adds a canvas to this document. ⌘N is unchanged. File › New
  Document from Starter… and ⇧⌘N on Home open the same picker in Home, and Create makes a
  document. Convert (⌥⌘T) reads the descriptors and looks the same.
- **A2 · Layout.** A sidebar (All, Recent, For You, the six categories), a grid of cards
  (picture, kicker, name, summary), a search field focused on open, Cancel and Create.
  Blank (a mind map, what ⌘N makes) is first.
- **A3 · Keyboard.** Typing filters (names, aliases, categories) while the arrow keys move
  the selection; ↩ creates, Esc cancels, a double-click creates. The picker opens on the
  starter used last, so ⌘T ↩ is two keys. ⌥-click on + skips it.
- **A4 · Setting.** Settings › Editing › New documents › "Choose a starter for new
  canvases", on by default; off, ⌘T makes the last starter's canvas at once.
- **A5 · Create.** One undo step; the canvas opens with a caret on its root. A Blank canvas
  is what ⌘T made before M40.
- **A6 · Speed.** The picker lays out under 100 ms; the canvas appears under 150 ms after ↩.
- **A7 · Accessible.** Each card is one VoiceOver button: name, then summary and category.
  Hover motion follows Reduce Motion.
- **A8 · Agents.** MCP lists starters.
- **A9 · Quality.** `swift test` and `scripts/lint.sh` pass, and the app builds.

### Design

- `StarterGallery` (NexusCanvas, `@Observable`): section, query, selection, columns,
  purpose (canvas or document), role; `visible`, `search`, `move(dx:dy:)`, `choose`,
  `cancel`. Recent starters live in `starters.recent` (8, newest first).
- `StarterGalleryView`: the sheet (900 × 600) and Home's embedded form (chips instead of
  the sidebar).
- `CanvasController.addCanvas(from:)`: the coordinator every new-canvas path goes through.
- `StarterThumbnails`: `ImageExporter` renders a template's canvas to fit the card at the
  screen's scale, once per appearance.
- The Guided badge and Start with Guide wait for M41, which brings the guides.

### Known gaps

- The picker wasn't driven by hand in the running app; screenshots of the sheet (light,
  dark, For You, a search) and Home were rendered offscreen and checked.
- Thumbnails render on first sight, not when the app is idle: each draws on the main
  thread, one per run-loop turn, and stays cached for the session.
- No XCUITest for the + paths (the CI Mac still lacks Automation permission, an M5 gap).
- No Guided badge or Start with Guide yet: M41.
