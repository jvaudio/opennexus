# M48 · KaTeX-level math and chemistry

Phase 6 of `collaboration-and-reach.md`: full TeX math and mhchem chemistry in place of
the `/math` subset of 0.3, rendered as vector shapes on the canvas, in every export, in
Present and on iOS, with inline `$…$` and display `$$…$$` in topics and notes.

Decisions #13 (vendor MathJax with a small bridge) and #14 (measure the speech engine,
then decide; TeX source by default) were confirmed on Oct 30, 2026 (D420, D421). M47d,
the full web viewer, hasn't run; M48 doesn't need it, because the HTML viewer draws the
SVG export, where formulas are paths.

## What we know before starting

- `/math` in 0.3 is `MathText` in `RichFormats.swift`: a LaTeX subset (Greek letters,
  `\frac` as a slash, `^` and `_` as raised and lowered runs, a few symbols) built as
  attributed text. It never fails; anything it doesn't know shows as typed.
- Node text is measured by `NodeMeasurer` with the platform's string drawing (TextKit on
  the Mac, CoreText on iOS) and drawn by `NodeLayer` the same way; `StyledText` routes
  measuring and drawing for exporters. The SVG export writes text as live `<text>` with a
  glyph-path fallback; PNG, PDF and print are a raster of the scene (PDF at 3×, D97);
  Present draws the scene; the HTML viewer draws the SVG export.
- `SVGPath.parse` (NexusTheme, M2) tokenizes path data into strings and has no `T`.
  MathJax's paths are long (a formula is often tens of kilobytes), so parsing is on the
  hot path of every formula's first render.
- Nothing in the app runs JavaScript today. JavaScriptCore is on macOS and iOS; MathJax 4
  has a lite DOM adaptor for it, and its font's glyph ranges load on demand, which needs
  a loader the app doesn't have.
- Topic case (Settings › Appearance) is applied to the title text before drawing, which
  would change TeX (`\Frac`).
- Markdown export writes `/math` topics' source as text; the Markdown importer has no
  maths; Xmind exports `/math` as an equation topic (`XmindExport.swift`) and imports it
  back. Word export (`Packages.swift`) lives in NexusInterchange, which NexusScene
  depends on, so it can't call the engine directly.
- There is no Acknowledgements page: About shows the version and the licence.

## Plan

### Engine (NexusScene/Math)

- **MathJax 4.1.3**, pinned (D420), with `@mathjax/mathjax-newcm-font` 4.1.3, its
  mhchem font extension 4.1.3, and mhchemparser 4.2.1, built by esbuild from `web/math`
  into one script, `Sources/NexusScene/MathJax/nexus-math.js`, with MathJax's `LICENSE`
  and a `NOTICE`. `scripts/build-math.sh` builds and copies it; `--check` (run by
  `scripts/lint.sh`) fails when the copy is stale.
- **TeX packages:** every one KaTeX's functions need, mhchem and cancel among them; not
  `require` or `autoload` (no loader), `noerrors` and `noundefined` (an error must be an
  error), or `physics` (not in KaTeX). KaTeX's `\htmlClass` and friends map to their
  content. Output is SVG with no glyph cache and no inline line breaking (the app places
  each formula whole).
- **Fonts:** the glyph ranges formulas use are bundled and loaded at start (Latin,
  double-struck, fraktur, script, sans, mono, calligraphic, maths, symbols, arrows,
  accents, shapes, variants); the rest (other scripts, bold and italic accented letters,
  braille) come back as `<text>`, drawn from the system's fonts as glyph outlines.
- **`MathEngine`:** one `JSContext` on a serial queue, started lazily (the apps warm it
  up at launch, off the main thread). An LRU cache of 4,000 results keyed by source and
  display mode; size and colour are applied at draw time. On the main thread of the apps
  a miss returns nil and typesets in the background; finished formulas post
  `didTypeset` (coalesced), and the canvas rebuilds. Exports, tests and command-line
  tools typeset synchronously (`MathEngine.waiting`).
- **`MathSVGReader`** turns MathJax's SVG into `MathGlyphs`: paths in ems, y down, with
  the baseline at 0, plus width, ascent and depth, and per-shape colours from `\color`.
  A byte-level path parser (`MathPathData`) replaces `SVGPath.parse` here.

### Formulas in text

- **Markup:** `$…$` inline and `$$…$$` display, by Pandoc's rules (an opening `$` has a
  non-space after it; a closing `$` has a non-space before it and no digit after it), so
  "$5 and $10" stays text. `\$` is a dollar sign. Settings › Editing › "Typeset math in
  text", on by default; off, the text shows as typed.
- **In styled text** a formula is one U+FFFC carrying a `MathRun` (glyphs, source,
  display, size). A display formula gets a centred line of its own. The formula's size
  matches the text's x-height. A formula still typesetting shows its source; one with an
  error shows its source in the error colour (the theme's critical red, lifted to 4.5:1
  on the topic) and carries MathJax's message.
- **`MathLayout`** lays out text that holds formulas with CoreText on every platform
  (each formula's room is a run delegate; lines grow for tall formulas and words stay
  centred in the line height), for both measuring and drawing, so a topic measures and
  draws the same everywhere. Text without formulas takes the existing path, unchanged.
- **`/math` topics** are one display formula. Topic case leaves formulas alone.

### Editing

- **Live preview:** while a topic with maths is edited, its typeset form shows beneath
  the editor (Mac and iOS), updated as you type.
- **Errors:** hovering a formula with an error shows MathJax's message (Mac); on iOS it is
  the accessibility value and shows in the preview.
- **`/chem`** in the slash menu makes the topic `/math` with `\ce{}` and the caret inside.

### Notes and Present

- The note popover previews a note's formulas beneath it; Present's presenter notes and
  the PDF's notes appendix typeset them. The HTML viewer keeps notes as text.

### Exports

- **SVG:** formulas as `<path>` elements in a `<g class="math">`, in the text's colour.
- **PDF and print:** the scene's raster with formulas left out, and each formula drawn
  over it as vector paths at its place (A3: "formulas as paths, no raster").
- **PNG, Present, iOS:** drawn from the same glyphs.
- **Word:** each formula an image (SVG with a PNG fallback, as Word expects), through a
  hook NexusCanvas installs in NexusInterchange.
- **Markdown:** `/math` topics export as `$$…$$`; a topic or line that is one `$$…$$`
  imports as `/math`; inline maths stays in the text both ways.
- **Xmind** keeps the equation round-trip of 0.3; OPML and plain text keep the source.

### Accessibility

- A formula's VoiceOver label is its TeX source (inline maths reads in place; a `/math`
  topic is "Equation, …"), unless MathJax's speech engine fits a budget of 1 MB added to
  the app (#14, D421): measured in this milestone.
- The contrast tests include the error colour on every theme's topic fills.

### Acknowledgements

- Settings › About gains Acknowledgements (Mac and iOS): MathJax's NOTICE and licence.

### Tests

- **NexusSceneTests:** the engine (typesets, versions, errors, the cache, async and
  waiting); the SVG reader (transforms, colours, rules, text fallback); the markup
  (Pandoc's cases, escapes, display, plain text); `MathLayout` (baseline, line growth,
  centring, wrapping); A6's 1,000 malformed inputs; the warm-render timing.
- **A1's corpus:** `Tests/Fixtures/math/katex-functions.txt`, one line per command or
  environment from KaTeX's supported-functions list, every line typeset without error;
  40 formulas against SVG goldens in `Tests/Fixtures/math/goldens/`.
- **A2:** every `/math` input in 0.3's tests and sample documents renders; 0.3's
  documents round-trip byte for byte.
- **NexusCanvasTests:** rendering topics and notes with maths; the setting off; SVG and
  PDF exports (paths, no images over formulas); Markdown, Xmind and Word; VoiceOver
  labels; the 200-formula open time; key to caret with maths.
- **iOS hosted:** the corpus on the iPhone 17 Pro and iPad simulators, compared with the
  Mac's glyphs.

### Changed from the plan

- The fixtures are in `Tests/NexusSceneTests/Math` (the corpus, `goldens.txt` and
  `Goldens/`), beside the tests that read them, not `Tests/Fixtures/math`; the iOS
  hosted suite reads the same files.
- Xmind: 0.3 didn't round-trip `/math` as an equation, as "What we know" said; it wrote
  the bare source and reported it unconverted. M48 writes `$$…$$` and reads it back.
- A 0.3 document gains `render/` in its `.gitignore` when saved, from M43; the round trip
  is byte for byte otherwise.
- Display formulas take the spaces beside them away, which the plan didn't say.
- `\includegraphics` is left out of the corpus and the layer (D423).

### Defects found

- **Inline formulas were cut short**: MathJax 4 splits inline maths at its break points
  into several `<svg>`s, and the app drew the first, so "E = mc²" showed as "E"
  (`linebreaks.inline: false`, D422).
- **mhchem's arrows were missing**: they are in a font extension of their own, now
  bundled (D422).
- **One formula's `\def` changed the next** (`\def\begin{matrix}` broke every later
  matrix): MathJax keeps definitions for the page (D425).
- **Table rules, frames and dashed lines were invisible**: their widths are in MathJax's
  CSS, which the reader now applies (D427).
- **Spaces beside a display formula** made blank-looking lines on the canvas and in Word.
- **Key to caret was 16.9 ms with formulas**: every topic layer restyled its title on
  each change (D439).
- **The iOS app didn't build**: importing NexusScene for the engine hid SwiftUI's `Scene`;
  the app imports only `MathEngine` from it.
- **Tests waited on each other**: on the shared engine, the corpus held up main-thread
  tests that typeset, and with them the background test's notice; the corpus suite has an
  engine of its own (D440).

### Known gaps

- The slides PDF draws each slide as a picture, formulas included (D434).
- iOS timings are the simulator's; JavaScriptCore on a real iPhone or iPad, where it may
  run with its JIT, is unmeasured. The Mac's JavaScriptCore runs a plain loop about 8×
  slower than Node.
- The HTML viewer's notes and the Outline view show formulas as source (D433).
- VoiceOver reads TeX, not speech (D437).
