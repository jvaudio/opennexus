# M25 · Xmind style fidelity

## Plan

**Goal (Xmind parity, from M18's and M23's known gaps):** close three gaps in the Xmind
exporters and importer.

1. Boundary colour, shape and dash weren't written to Xmind, and the importer read no
   boundary style at all.
2. Round trips flattened styling: level styles and quick styles came back as each
   topic's own style, and `branch` colours as fixed hex.
3. The theme's boundary, summary and relationship classes weren't imported.

`.nexus` files are unchanged: the model gains nothing, and both schema copies stay as
they are.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Boundaries:** a boundary's colour, fill opacity, dash, width and shape, and a
  summary's colour, width and bracket, survive `.xmind` and XMind 8 round trips, written
  in Xmind's own keys.
- **A2 · Level and quick styles:** after a round trip, level styles are level styles,
  a built-in quick style is the topic's quick style, and a style in the branch colour
  still says `branch`. Xmind still gets the resolved properties.
- **A3 · Edits in Xmind win:** a topic whose style was changed after OpenNexus wrote it
  imports with Xmind's style, not the stale record.
- **A4 · Theme classes:** another app's boundary, summary and relationship classes
  import onto each element, under the element's own style. A theme OpenNexus wrote
  doesn't stamp its defaults onto every element.
- **A5 · Unchanged files:** an Xmind file without OpenNexus's record imports as before.

### Approach

- **Boundaries and summaries:** the same property mapping the topics and relationships
  use (D185), plus the theme's classes rewritten to hold OpenNexus's defaults, so each
  element only writes what it changes.
- **OpenNexus's record:** an `org.opennexus.style` extension, which Xmind keeps but
  ignores, on the sheet (levels, theme) and on topics (quick style, unresolved own
  style), each topic's guarded by a fingerprint of the properties written.
- **Theme classes:** each element's properties merged over its class before mapping.
  There's no canvas-level default to put them in, and none was added (D221).

### Built

- **`XmindStyle`** (D217–D220):
  - Boundary and summary vocabularies, `properties(_: Boundary …)`,
    `properties(_: Summary …)` and `apply` for both.
  - The theme's `boundary`, `summary` and `relationship` classes hold OpenNexus's
    defaults. The boundary class was dashed; the canvas draws boundaries solid.
  - `record` / `restore` for a topic's record, `record(_: CanvasStyle …)`,
    `recordedTheme` and `levels` for a sheet's, and `fingerprint`.
  - `elementClasses` and `isOwn`. `canvasStyle` takes the sheet's record (level styles,
    an untagged theme) instead of a report.
- **`XmindWriter`:** boundaries and summaries get `style.properties`; topics and sheets
  get the record when it adds something.
- **XMind 8 writer:** `style-id` on boundaries and summaries, a `summary` theme family,
  and records as JSON text in `<extensions>` on the sheet and topics.
- **Importer (Zen and XMind 8):** reads boundary and summary styles once the sheet's
  background is known, merges the theme's element classes, and restores records.
- **`ICanvas.Group`** (colour, fill opacity, dash, width, shape), **`ICanvas.Brace`**
  (colour, width, bracket) and **`ITopic.quickStyle`**, applied by `CanvasBuilder`.

### Known gaps

- **Unverified in Xmind itself.** No Xmind was available, so these are taken on trust:
  - that Xmind keeps the `org.opennexus.style` extension through a save;
  - that it doesn't rewrite a topic's properties when nothing changed (if it does,
    every record goes stale and topics fall back to their properties: the M18 result);
  - Xmind's summary shape class names;
  - how XMind 8 treats an extension on a sheet.

  `XmindRealFileTests` with a map saved by Xmind after an OpenNexus export is the check.
- **New elements after an import** use the OpenNexus theme's boundary, summary and
  relationship defaults, not the Xmind theme's (D221). **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D245).**
- **Not written:**
  - A custom quick style by name: it's merged into the topic's own style (D219).
  - A relationship coloured by its source.
  - A boundary's technical meaning, trust level and title position (reported).
- **Approximated:**
  - Stadium, ellipse and corners boundaries, and line brackets, are written as near
    shapes.
  - Xmind's tension boundary reads as rounded.
  - A boundary fill that isn't a tint of its line colour reads as the nearest tint.
- **Still not imported:** theme topic classes on XMind 8 files, and Xmind's
  `importantTopic`, `minorTopic` and `expiredTopic` classes (M23). **Fixed 2026-10-23
  (D243, D244).**
