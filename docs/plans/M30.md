# M30 · Fishbone sub-causes

## Plan

**Goal (closing M29's first known gap):** OpenNexus's fishbone layout stacked a cause's
sub-causes under it without making room, so they overlapped the next cause on the bone
(drawn "Staffing" over "New hires"), and its exports with sub-causes didn't read back.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · No overlaps:** a fishbone with several causes per bone, some with sub-causes and
  one with a sub-cause of its own, lays out with no two topics within 4 pt of each other,
  head on either side. The test fails before the fix.
- **A2 · Clear of the bone:** sub-causes, and the lines to them, stay clear of their bone,
  on lower bones too, where the bone leans toward them.
- **A3 · Unchanged elsewhere:** every existing structure test and the image-to-map bench
  (`ImageOutlineTests`, `ImagePagesTests`, `ImageVisionTests`, round trips) still pass.
- **A4 · Round trip:** OpenNexus's own fishbone export with sub-causes reads back exact,
  head on either side.

## Results (2026-10-22)

| | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | **Met.** `StructureTests.fishboneSubCausesDoNotOverlap` failed on the old layout on both sides: sub-causes on the next cause along the bone, and the last cause's sub-causes across the spine on the facing bone's cause. It passes now (D240). |
| A2 | **Met.** The same test checks every sub-cause's end nearest the bone is at least 10 pt from it, top and bottom; it fails with no clearance. The `fishboneSub` line drops beside the stack. Checked by eye on exports with four bones, both sides. |
| A3 | **Met.** Causes without sub-causes keep at least the old spacing; causes taller than it allowed (30 pt ones, as the canvas draws them) now sit 6 pt apart instead of nearly touching. The four fishbone round trips and the drawn sub-cause bench read back as before. |
| A4 | **Met** with D241. `ImageRoundTripTests.exportedFishboneWithSubCausesReadsBack`: four bones, sub-causes on upper and lower bones, one with a sub-cause of its own, exact with real Vision, both sides. |

### Built

- **NexusLayout:** `StructureLayouts.bone` (causes' places along a bone and its length),
  `Bone`, `causeGap`, `boneClearance`; `fishbone` places each cause's sub-causes from
  that and spaces pairs of bones by what they hold. `BranchLines` family `fishboneSub`.
- **NexusInterchange:** `Builder.stacked` allows two lines.

### Tests

560 test functions, all passing (`swift test`); `swift format lint --strict` clean;
the app builds with `xcodebuild`. New this milestone (2):
- `StructureTests.fishboneSubCausesDoNotOverlap` (both sides).
- `ImageRoundTripTests.exportedFishboneWithSubCausesReadsBack` (real Vision, both sides).

### Known gaps

- **Sub-causes are stacked, not drawn as small bones** branching off their cause's rib.
  Xmind can draw deeper levels that way, and image import doesn't read that shape either
  (M29). **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D268, D270).**
- **On a lower bone, a tall stack moves out** from the bone by half its height, so a
  cause with many sub-causes there widens the diagram. **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D268).**
- **A cause on an upper bone** can touch its bone at the cause's top corner nearest the
  bone, where the bone leans toward it (as before M30). **Fixed 2026-10-23 (D269).**
- **Not tried by hand in the running app;** checked on PNG exports from `nexus export`,
  which use the same layout and drawing.
