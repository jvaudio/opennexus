# M15 · Zone relationships

## Plan

**Goal (closing D158):** a relationship connects a topic or a zone to a topic or a zone,
as Xmind 26.03 allows.

**Accept when:**
- A relationship can be drawn from a topic to a zone with Connect mode.
- It survives save and load, and undo.
- It renders on the canvas and in exports: PNG, JPEG, PDF, SVG, slides, and a zone's own
  export.
- It imports from Xmind.

### Model

- **`RelationshipEnd`** (NexusCore/Node.swift): `.topic(NodeID)` or `.zone(String)`.
  `Relationship.from` and `.to` are ends. Helpers: `topics` (both ends, when both are
  topics), `endTopics`, `endZones`, `touches(_:)`, `touches(zone:)` and
  `connects(within:)`. `Canvas.has(_:)` checks either kind of end.
- **File format:** `from` and `to` still hold the id. `fromKind` / `toKind` are written
  only as `"zone"` (schema: enum `topic`, `zone`). Files without them read as before.
- **Housekeeping:** `validate()` reports a relationship to a missing topic or zone,
  `repair()` drops it, a three-way merge drops relationships whose zone the other side
  removed, and `removeZone` removes the zone's relationships in the same undo step.

### Canvas

- **Scene:** zones are built before relationships, so a relationship's end can be a zone.
  `Scene.rect(of:)` gives a topic's frame or a zone's rectangle.
- **Routing:** `RelationshipGeometry.make` takes the zone's rectangle like a topic's
  frame. When one rectangle holds the other (a member connected to its own zone), the
  line runs to the zone's nearest edge (`RelationshipGeometry.nested`). The anchor
  follows a zone while it's dragged.
- **Connect mode:** zones are candidates with hint chips at their corners, ranked with
  the topics by centre distance. The text filter matches "Zone: title". With a zone
  selected, ⇧⌘L starts from the zone.
- **Exports:** SVG and the Convert sheet's wireframe use the same rectangles; slides
  render through the scene. A zone export keeps a relationship whose ends are the zone
  or its members (D155).
- **Accessibility:** a relationship reads "Pricing to zone Later". The Connect rotor
  lists zones with their hints, and the outline and "read edges" setting name zone ends.

### Interchange and agents

- **Xmind:** a relationship naming a boundary in floating branches makes that boundary a
  zone around those branches. One in the main tree is reported as "Relationship to a
  boundary".
- **Mermaid** flowcharts use `subgraph zone_<id>`; **PlantUML** component diagrams use
  `rectangle "Title" as zone_<id> { … }`; **draw.io** gets a `zone_<id>` cell behind
  the topics. Sequence, class and DFD output, and their rules, use topic-to-topic
  relationships only.
- **Agent tools:** `add_edge` takes `zone:<id or title>` for either end. `read_map`
  lists zones as `Title [zone:z1]` and writes relationship ends the same way.

## Results

| Criterion | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Drawn from a topic to a zone with Connect mode | ✅ `ConnectTests.connectToAndFromAZone`: a hint, the title filter and a zone as the source. |
| Survives save and load, and undo | ✅ JSON round trip (byte-identical), an older file without kinds, and undo of the connection and of removing the zone. The random undo property tests now connect to zones and remove them. |
| Renders and exports | ✅ Anchors on the zone's edge from outside and from a member, follow a zone move, SVG with zone metadata, and the zone-scoped export keeping only its own relationships. |
| Imports from Xmind | ✅ A boundary around a floating branch becomes a zone end; a main-tree boundary is reported. Xmind 26.03's own zone records aren't read (see Known gaps). |

### Tests

New this milestone:
- `ElementTests.zoneRelationshipEnds`: JSON keys, round trip, an older file, removing the
  zone and its undo, validation and repair of a missing zone, and a merge where the disk
  removed the zone.
- `UndoPropertyTests`: random operations now include connecting a topic and a zone (in
  either direction) and removing a zone. Invariants hold and every step undoes.
- `ElementSceneTests.relationshipToAZone`: the scene, both anchor cases, following a
  zone move, save and load, the zone-scoped export, SVG metadata, and removing the zone.
- `ConnectTests.connectToAndFromAZone`.
- `ZoneRelationshipInterchangeTests`: Xmind boundary ends, plus Mermaid, PlantUML and
  draw.io zone output, which re-imports.
- `MCPTests.zoneEnds`: `zone:` references and the `read_map` outline.

### Known gaps

- **Xmind 26.03 zones aren't imported.** OpenNexus never read them (this milestone
  doesn't change that): their JSON isn't documented and there was no sample file. A
  relationship to one is reported as "Relationship to a missing topic". When a sample
  turns up, read the zones into `ICanvas` and resolve their ids like boundary ids.
- **Relationships to boundaries, summaries or callouts** (Xmind allows these too) are
  still topic or zone only.
- **Copying a zone** isn't possible yet, so pasting never brings zone-ended relationships.
- **Mermaid and draw.io imports** turn a subgraph or container into a boundary, so an
  edge to it re-imports as an edge to a new topic named after it.
