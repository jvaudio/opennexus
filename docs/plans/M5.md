# M5 · Views and navigation

## Plan

**Goal:**
- The ⌘K command palette and ⌘J quick jump.
- Outline and Split views with live-linked selection.
- ZEN, Focus (⌘.) and the filter bar.
- The navigation panel (⌃⌘1–5) with find and replace (regex, scope) and counts.
- Present mode: auto slides; S / M / H; aspect ratios (16:9 default); slide themes; a ? key list; presenter view; export to PDF, PowerPoint, Keynote and video.

**Accept when:** the views match artboards 25–27, 32 and 47.

### Principles

- **One controller, many views.** Canvas, outline, palette, jump, panels and present mode
  all read and drive the same `CanvasController`. Selection, editing and undo are shared,
  so "selection survives every switch" (artboard 27) and both panes stay live-linked
  (artboard 26) without syncing code.
- **View state is not document state.** These change only what the scene shows: focus,
  filter dimming, search highlights, tag highlights and the present slide. They don't
  touch the model, so they never enter undo. The document stores only:
  - saved filters;
  - each topic's "hide from slides" flag;
  - the deck settings (order, skips, merges, transition, aspect, theme).

### Model (NexusCore)

- `NodeFilter`: clauses (`field op value`) parsed from chip text.
  - Fields: `type`, `risk`, `status`, `marker`, `label`, `text`, `todo`, `note`, `ai`.
  - Operators: `:` / `=` / `≠` / `!=`, combined with AND.
  - Evaluation runs against a canvas. Saved filters live on the canvas (`filters`).
- `TextSearch`: options for case, whole words, regex, this canvas or all canvases, and
  whether to include notes and labels. Matches carry canvas, node, field (text, note or
  label) and range. Replace one or replace all are each a single undo step per canvas.
- `CanvasCounts`: topics, words, characters, notes and AI-created, for the canvas and
  for the selection.
- `Deck`: slides generated from a canvas.
  - Generation rules:
    - Hierarchy maps get a title slide plus one slide per main topic (sub-topics shown).
    - Flowcharts and DFDs with "Step through flow" get one slide per flow in
      breadth-first order: the source, target and flow are highlighted, and the slide
      is titled from the flow's label.
    - Sequences get one slide per message.
  - `PresentationSettings` on the canvas: skipped slides, merges, custom order,
    transition (glide, zoom, cut), aspect (16:9, 4:3, 16:10, 1:1), theme (canvas, dark,
    light) and step-through-flow.
  - Each topic carries a `presentHidden` flag (H).

### Controller and views (NexusCanvas)

- **Command palette (⌘K, artboard 25):**
  - Rows are generated from the registry; commands that can't run are *hidden*.
  - Sections: Commands, AI · on-device, Settings (Shortcuts › … → Rebind).
  - Dynamic rows: "Select by type · X" for each node type present.
  - Ranked recent first, then fuzzy match. Each row shows its standard and Hyper keys.
  - Keys: ↑↓, ↩ runs, ⌘↩ runs and keeps the palette open, ⇥ shows the note, Esc.
- **Quick jump (⌘J):**
  - Searches topics, edges (relationships and labelled flows) and canvases across the
    document.
  - Each row shows a colour dot, a path breadcrumb and a type badge.
  - ↩ flies the camera there (400 ms) and selects; ⇧↩ adds to the selection.
- **Outline (⌘2) and Split (⌘3), artboard 26:**
  - An AppKit outline view drawn from the same tree: bullets in branch colours, notes
    inline, relationships ("→ links to Timeline (“drives”)"), and "N hidden children ·
    folded" lines.
  - Its own key scope: ↩ sibling, ⇥ indent, ⇧⇥ outdent, ⌥⌘↑↓ reorder, ↑↓ move, ← → fold.
  - It shares the node editor. Clicking a note edits it inline.
  - Split puts the outline on the left and the canvas on the right.
- **ZEN (⌥⌘F):** full screen with every toolbar and panel hidden and a faint "N topics ·
  N words" count. Esc exits; all keys still work.
- **Focus (⌘.):**
  - The focused branch is laid out as the whole map at its own place and drawn with the
    root's look. The camera flies to it; everything else blurs and dims.
  - A breadcrumb shows the way back; Esc climbs out one level. ⌘. on a DFD process
    still drills into its level (M4).
- **Filter bar (⇧⌘F):**
  - Chips (`type: Threat`, `risk: High`, `status ≠ Mitigated`, `+ marker, label,
    text…`), a Dim · Hide toggle and an "N of M match" count.
  - "Save as …" stores the filter on the canvas; saved filters appear in the navigation
    panel and can drive Present.
- **Navigation panel (⌃⌘1–5, artboard 47):**
  - Search tab (⌘F): find and replace with case, whole words, regex, this or all
    canvases, +Notes, +Labels, ⌥↩ / ⌥⌘↩, and a result list with paths. ↩ / ⇧↩ steps
    through matches; matches glow on the canvas, with the substring highlighted.
  - Outline tab: a compact tree to jump through.
  - Notes tab: every note in order.
  - Tags tab: markers and labels with counts. Click highlights related topics and fades
    the rest to 30%; ⇧-click combines.
  - To-dos tab: Open / Done / All, with checkboxes that toggle.
- **Counts:** a zoom control with "N topics" bottom-right; clicking it opens the counts
  card (this canvas and the selection).
- **Present (⇧⌘P, artboard 32):**
  - The stage frames the slide's topics at the chosen aspect and dims the rest. A
    kicker ("CHECKOUT · DATA FLOW · STEP 3 OF 5"), the slide title and progress dots sit
    over it.
  - The slide strip shows wireframe thumbnails (drag to reorder), transition chips,
    step-through-flow, aspect, theme, the ? key list and Export.
  - Keys: → / ← next and previous, S skip, M merge with next, H hide the topic, F
    full-screen stage, ? keys, Esc end.
  - Presenter view opens on a second display when there is one (else as a window): a
    timer, the next slide and the topic's note.
  - Export:
    - PDF: one vector-quality page per slide.
    - PowerPoint: .pptx, one full-bleed image per slide, with notes.
    - Keynote: the same .pptx, which Keynote opens.
    - Video: H.264 .mp4 at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 3 s per slide, with 0.6 s cross-fades.

Defects found during M5 review and fixed:
- The hidden full-size present overlay collapsed the canvas host to zero size; the style goldens caught it.
- A new sibling's outline row didn't exist yet when the outline looked for it to edit.
- Overlays followed the system's dark appearance over light canvases; they now follow the canvas theme.
- The DFD panel and violation card covered the slide title and strip during Present.
- The selection ring showed on slides and in exports.
- ⇧/ didn't count as "?".
- The jump header pluralised "1 nodes".
- The status bar hid under the navigation panel.
