# M36 · Fishbone ribs and image import

## Plan

**Goal (closing M29's and M30's known gaps):** OpenNexus draws a fishbone's sub-causes the
way Xmind does, each on its own small rib off its cause's line, with nothing touching its
bone; image import reads that shape, wrapped sub-causes and lower-bone sub-causes; and
File › Import… shows its window for any import that takes a while, a single picture too.
Everything still runs on this Mac (D150): Vision on device, no network, no new
dependencies.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Small ribs:** every sub-cause sits on its own rib, a line under it to a small bone
  that leaves its parent's rib and runs parallel to the main bone, nested for sub-causes of
  sub-causes; no two topics come within 4 pt; head on either side.
- **A2 · Lower bones and corners:** on a lower bone, a cause with many sub-causes doesn't
  push them out as a block; causes clear their bone at both corners.
- **A3 · Reading the ribs:** OpenNexus's own fishbone exports with sub-causes read back
  exact with real Vision (`exportedFishboneWithSubCausesReadsBack`, both sides), and so do
  boxes laid out along small bones, mirrored too.
- **A4 · Wrapped and lower-bone sub-causes:** a sub-cause wrapped onto a second line that
  starts with a capital reads as one, in an export and on boxes; a lower-bone sub-cause set
  back less than ¾ of a line reads as a sub-cause when the picture shows the causes' ribs.
- **A5 · Import window:** a single picture still importing after half a second asks for the
  progress window once, which says "Grouping…" with an indeterminate bar while the model
  works; Cancel abandons it. A picture read at once opens with no window.
- **A6 · Unchanged elsewhere:** every image bench case (`ImageOutlineTests`,
  `ImagePagesTests`, `ImageVisionTests`, the round trips) and structure test passes.

### Small ribs (D268, D269)

- **`StructureLayouts.ribs`** lays a parent's sub-causes out from the point where their
  small bone leaves the parent's rib (`ribInset`, 10 pt in from the parent's end nearest
  the bone): each 6 pt below the one before, its end `boneOffset(height)` from the small
  bone at its rib, and its own sub-causes hung from its rib the same way.
- **`boneOffset`** (`6 + ½(height − 2)`) places every cause and sub-cause, so the top
  corner clears a bone leaning toward it by 6 pt.
- **`BranchLines` `fishboneSub`**: the small bone from the parent's rib to the sub-cause's
  rib, then the rib under the sub-cause to its far end.

### Reading them (D270, D271)

- **`subCauseFit`** tries the sub-cause fit straight down and along each bone slope, keeps
  readings whose bones have that slope, and picks the most sub-causes, then the fewest
  nested, then straight down.
- **`ruled(under:)`** looks for a rib in the picture's ink: a sub-cause tucked under a ruled
  cause with no rib of its own is a sub-cause at any distance, and **`joinRuled`** joins a
  line to the one above when only it is ruled.

### Import window (D272)

- **`ImageImportProgress`** reports `Step`s (`.pages`, `.grouping`), asks for its window via
  `onShow` once (two or more pages, or still running after `showAfter`, 0.5 s), and is
  `indeterminate` for one page or while grouping. `ImageToMap.importImage(grouping:)`.
- **`ReadingProgressBar`** takes an optional value: nil sweeps, or stays still and empty
  with Reduce Motion.

### Known gaps

- **Sub-causes hang below their cause on both sides.** On a lower bone they follow the
  bone outward as they go down, as the causes below them do; a list there could hang
  above its cause instead, toward the spine, but image import (D237) reads sub-causes
  below their cause.
- **Ribs are read from the ink only as present or absent** under a line. A sub-cause
  whose rib reaches its main bone, or a picture whose causes have no ribs, falls back to
  distance from the bone (D237).
- **A wrapped sub-cause in a picture without ink** (or one too dark to trace, D193) still
  reads as two when its second line starts with a capital.
- **Not tried by hand in the running app:** the import window for a single picture and
  the "Grouping…" phase (Group plain lists needs Apple Intelligence on this Mac); checked
  at the controller and on PNG exports.
