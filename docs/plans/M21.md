# M21 · Present and legend gaps

## Plan

**Goal (closing gaps from M5, M11 and M14):**
- **The legend in Present** (M14's out-of-scope list): when the canvas shows a legend,
  Present shows it too, with a toggle, and slide exports carry it.
- **Task details in Present** (M11's "not covered"): a slide whose topic is a task shows
  its task line. Optionally, a Timeline slide draws the branch as a compact Gantt.
- **Moving the legend from the keyboard** (M14's known gap): with the legend selected,
  arrows nudge it and ⇧ moves it further. VoiceOver can move it, and every action is a
  registry command.

**Accept when:**
- **A1 · Legend in Present:** slides show the legend as the setting says, the setting has
  a toggle in Present's strip, and PDF, PowerPoint and video slides draw it.
- **A2 · Task details:** task slides show the task line and follow Settings › Show task
  details on topics. Timeline slides reuse M11's `GanttLayout` and `GanttRenderer`.
- **A3 · Keyboard and VoiceOver:** arrows and ⇧-arrows move the selected legend, one undo
  step per press. Reset Position stays. VoiceOver has move actions. Each action is a
  registry command with a key, a menu item or a palette row.
- **A4 · Quality bars:** strings are localised, the format additions are optional and
  round-trip, there are no conflicts in any key profile, and the accessibility audit is
  clean.

### Legend in Present: a corner, not a closing slide (D200)

- **Choice:** a corner overlay on every slide. The legend explains markers where they
  appear, and a closing slide would come after the audience needed it. The box's place on
  the canvas is cropped by each slide's framing, so the canvas's own box steps aside while
  presenting (`SceneViewState.hideLegend`).
- **Where:** bottom-left, at the same margin as the progress dots on the right. It's
  1.2× the canvas size on screen and 2× on a 1920 × 1080 exported slide, never taller than
  about 40% of the slide.
- **Setting:** `presentation.legend`, on by default and written only when off. The
  strip's **Legend ✓** chip (shown when the canvas has a visible legend) and **L** toggle
  it, as one undo step.
- **Contents:** the canvas legend's rows (`SceneLegend.make`). Timeline slides leave it out.

### Task details (D201, D202)

- **Task line:** the slide's own topic (a branch's main topic, a flow's target, or the
  central topic on the title slide) shows `TaskFormat.line` under the title, such as
  "Ana · 2 Nov → 6 Nov · 40%". The topics on the stage already show their lines, because
  the stage is the canvas's scene. Settings › Show task details on topics turns off both.
- **Timeline slides:** `presentation.timeline` (off by default; the strip's
  **Timeline slides** chip, shown when the canvas has a dated task, or **T**).
  - One follows each branch slide whose branch has a dated task (`timeline:<topic>`), so
    skip, merge and reorder work on it.
  - It's drawn by `GanttRenderer` from `GanttLayout(only:)`, clipped to the branch's own
    dates (±2 days, or weeks past 45 days) with the table pinned. On screen it fills the
    stage under the title; in exports it sits below the title at up to 2.4×.
  - It's titled "Build · Timeline" in the slide, the strip and the presenter view.

### Moving the legend (D203, D204)

- **Selecting it:** View › Select Legend (and the palette), a click on the box, or
  VoiceOver focus. It draws with the selection ring.
- **Keys** (a new `legend` scope, like `gantt`):

  | Command | Key |
  | --- | --- |
  | Move Legend Left / Right / Up / Down | ← → ↑ ↓ (10 pt) |
  | Move Legend Far Left / Right / Up / Down | ⇧← ⇧→ ⇧↑ ⇧↓ (50 pt) |
  | Edit Selected Legend | ↩ |
  | Done Moving Legend | Esc |
  | Select Legend, Reset Legend Position | View menu, palette |

  Other canvas keys fall through to the topics and end the legend's selection. So does
  selecting a topic or an element, or a click elsewhere.
- **Undo:** one step per press. That matches Move Up and the Gantt's day moves, since
  topics have no spatial nudge to copy.
- **VoiceOver:** the legend element adds Move Legend Left / Right / Up / Down and, once
  moved, Reset Legend Position. Focusing it selects it, selecting it moves VoiceOver's
  focus there, and its frame is read live. In Present, the corner legend and the
  Timeline chart are image elements with labels (D137).

### Decisions to record (D200–D204)

1. The legend as a corner overlay, with its setting.
2. The task line under the slide title.
3. Timeline slides.
4. The legend's key scope, selection and undo.
5. VoiceOver move actions and focus.

### Built

- **NexusCore:** `PresentationSettings.legend` and `.timeline`; `Slide.timeline`; Timeline
  slides in `Deck.generated`.
- **NexusKeys:** the `legend` scope; Select Legend, Reset Legend Position, and ten
  legend-scope commands.
- **NexusCanvas:**
  - `PresentExtras.swift`: `presentLegend(for:)`, `slideTaskLine`, `slideTitle`,
    `timelineChart(for:)`, `TimelineChart`, `PresentLegendCorner`, `TimelineCard`, the
    L and T toggles, and `SlideExporter.drawExtras`.
  - `LegendKeyboard.swift`: selection, nudges and the handlers.
  - Small hooks in `Scene` (`hideLegend`), `CanvasView` (click to select, the ring, the
    VoiceOver focus), `CanvasController` (the scope and key routing), `LegendLayer`
    (the ring) and `LegendItem` (the actions).
  - `SlideExporter.renderSlides` takes optional `prefs` and `today` for tests.

### Known gaps

- **The corner legend can cover topics** on a slide that frames content in its
  bottom-left corner. The stage doesn't reserve room for it; turn it off with L.
- **The legend lists the whole canvas on every slide**, not only the markers on that
  slide.
- **Timeline slides follow main topics only:** flowchart, DFD and sequence decks have no
  branches, so they get no Timeline slides.
- **The Present strip's chips are mouse targets**, like M5's other strip controls; the
  keyboard uses L and T.
- **README §11** has no M21 entry; the coordinator adds it.
